# Session 4: The Sleeping God

**Date Played:** 2026-03-07

## Summary
Beneath the Chryseum in [[Aurelion]], [[Sir Roderic Lightbearer|Roderic]] languished in a church dungeon — captured by the Aureate after sending a Luciferian priest to carry word to [[Beaconhold]] about the return of the old gods and [[Count Albrecht Marrow]]'s corruption.
The dungeon held dozens of prisoners: bards, performers, and suspected followers of the Laughing One, all imprisoned under [[Luminary Severin Morrow]]'s orders.
[[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]] and [[Castor]] tracked [[Captain Eisen Dorn]] through the tunnels to find Roderic, and Castor shapeshifted into a mouse — retaining beaver-like features from his imperfect transformation — to steal the guards' keys.
In the chaos that followed, Harlequin prisoners were freed and promptly ordered back into their cells by Roderic's divine decree, while Garland shattered a guard's sword with [[Second Harvest]].
The party reunited, though [[Edric Bloom|Edric]] remained missing, having vanished while under an invisibility spell during the approach through the tunnels.

After barricading the cell block, the party emerged into the Chryseum's grand nave, where signs of both ruin and renewal manifested simultaneously: cracks formed in the stone while moss pushed up through the fractures, and torches dimmed before reigniting with a soft white holy light.
[[Dawnwarden Brenn]] and her [[Order of the First Dawn]] confronted them, but Roderic negotiated passage by invoking his honor as a Luciferian knight.
Brenn shared a radical theology — that Lucifer's suppression of ruin centuries ago had caused renewal to go dormant, leading to an age of stagnation with civilizations crumbling and nothing rising to replace them.

[[Luminary Severin Morrow]] then appeared and revealed the truth the party had been seeking: the Morrow family have been secret worshippers of renewal for generations, and he, his father [[Count Albrecht Marrow]], and [[Dawnwarden Brenn]] may be the heralds of renewal — the counterparts to the party's heralds of ruin.
He opened a hidden spiral staircase beneath the Chryseum floor, leading the party down into the [[Shrine of Renewal]], an ancient, pre-Luciferian chamber where a translucent cocoon held the sleeping form of the God of Renewal.
The heralds felt an interlocking connection — like puzzle pieces drawn together — but the pattern was incomplete; they needed Count Marrow to complete the balance.
Garland's spellbook responded to the residual renewal energy, its pages growing slightly stronger, offering the first real hope that his lost knowledge could be recovered.
As the combined heralds traveled back through the tunnels, a fragile equilibrium emerged — cracks sealed, moss grew to fill gaps — proof that ruin and renewal could coexist.
Yet as they emerged near Crest Aurelion to retrieve Marrow, they discovered the [[Order of the Eclipsed Sword]], the Church of Lucifer's own military order, had arrived to arrest the very man they needed most.
