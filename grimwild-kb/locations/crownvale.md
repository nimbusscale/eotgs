# Crownvale

**Type:** Town
**First Visited:** [[Session 0]]

## Description
The seat of power in the [[Ashen Vale]].
Ruled by [[Aldric Garlandsson]], [[Garland yn Greenholt]]'s grandson, who styles himself as a would-be petty king of the vale.

## Notable Features
- Political center of the [[Ashen Vale]]
- Lies directly in the path of the curse if the beaver dam on the [[Ashen Flow]] breaks

## Connected Locations
- [[Ashen Vale]] — The region Crownvale governs
- [[Ashen Flow]] — The river whose cursed waters threaten the town

## Associated NPCs
- [[Aldric Garlandsson]] — Ruler of Crownvale

## Events Here
- [[Session 0]] — Established as the seat of power in the Ashen Vale, at risk from the curse

## Notes
Crownvale and its people are the most immediate civilization at risk if the beaver dam fails.
