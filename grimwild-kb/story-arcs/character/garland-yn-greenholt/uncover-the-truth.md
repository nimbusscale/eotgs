# Uncover the Truth

**Type:** Character ([[Garland yn Greenholt]])
**Theme:** Discover the Truth
**Status:** Active
**Started:** [[Session 1]]

## Summary
[[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]] chose "Uncover the Truth" as his character arc, driven by suspicion that his grandson [[Aldric Garlandsson|Aldric]] may be connected to the events at [[Ashbrook]].
Evidence is mounting: [[Rowan yn Greenholt|Rowan]]'s letter mentioned someone in Aldric's livery near Ashbrook before the disaster, and a fresh scrap of Aldric's livery was found at [[Beaver Lake]].
However, [[Edric Bloom|Edric]]'s investigation of Aldric's guards suggests Aldric himself is uninvolved.
The mystery remains — who bearing his colors was present at these locations?

## Open Questions
- Who was wearing Aldric's livery at Beaver Lake and near Ashbrook before the disaster?
- Is Aldric connected to the events at Ashbrook, or is someone using his name?
- What is the full story behind the Seal of Unmaking's retrieval?
