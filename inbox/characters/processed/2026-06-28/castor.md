## Moves

### At One With The World
**Starting Move.** You can speak with animals and Scrutinize and Compel them. You also don't require food or drink.

### Attunement
**Attunement** **When you spend meaningful time observing, interacting with, or embodying a creature, you may attempt to attune with it. Roll+WIS.**

### Commune
**Starting Move.** When you spend some time in a place, connecting with nearby creatures and spirits, roll+WIS. You will be granted a vision of significance to you, your allies, or the spirits around you, the GM will describe it.

### Shapeshift
**Starting Move.** When you call upon the wilds to change your shape, choose one of your Attuned Creatures to change into and roll+WIS.

While in an animal shape, you gain any innate abilities and weaknesses of the form: claws, wings, gills, etc. You also gain the Instinct of your new form. This primal urge is something innate to each creature and becomes a part of you while you are shapeshifted.

You don't need to Shapeshift to change back into your normal shape, though you may need to Resist Instinct.

### Resist Instinct
**Starting Move.** When you try to change shape without having fulfilled your Instinct, roll+WIS.
