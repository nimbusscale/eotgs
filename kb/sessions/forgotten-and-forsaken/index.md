# The Forgotten and the Forsaken

**Sessions:** [[Session 6]]–
**Theme:** TBD
**Status:** Active

The cocoon opened and the world breathed, but balance is not peace.
Renewal has awakened within the Shrine, yet ruin's passage has left scars no ceremony can heal — and now something is being forgotten at a scale larger than the curse any one of them once carried.
Along the [[Bonewall]] the wards are failing, the faithful are vanishing, and the hero [[Sariel]] is being unmade from memory and record.

The party were scattered and converging on the Bonewall, each finding the same rot wearing a different face.

In [[Session 7]] the three threads finally met, deep inside the wall, in the hidden town of [[The Slate|the Slate]] — a people of living stone whose ageless, deathless, birthless stasis has just broken with the first child born among them in living memory.

In [[Session 8]] the memory stones of the Slate gave up the deep past — that the wall's wards were built by a deep dwarf, Vaalo, drawing on the light of a world below — and then [[Moldrex]] tore the hidden town in two over that newborn child.

## Sessions
- [[Session 6]] — *Three Roads to the Bonewall* — Roderic is charged to investigate the failing wards as the faithful vanish and Sariel is erased; Mira opens a Witch Stone portal onto the Bonewall and Garland and Castor follow; Paxton burns Senna's barge in the Reaches and learns the name Moldrex.
- [[Session 7]] — *The Town of the Slate* — All three threads converge underground in the town of the Slate, reuniting the party and joining Paxton in play; Roland's lantern-marked men try to keep Roderic from the buried Spinewright's tomb, the Bonewall's wilds mutate as the wards fail, and the Slate's broken stasis surfaces with their first child in an age.
- [[Session 8]] — *The Memory Stones* — The Slate's memory stones grant the party their tongue and reveal Vaalo the deep dwarf, his lantern Hiillos, and another figure erased from history like Sariel; Garland sacrifices the memory of his dead son to free Mira from the realm of the forgotten; and the town fractures as Moldrex's converts move to give the child to the dark god, his vines breaking through Muistola's ceiling as spiders swarm the wall outside.
