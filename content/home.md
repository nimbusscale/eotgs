## The World

The Old Gods once governed the natural cycles of the world — Ruin, Renewal, Death, the Hunt, the Forgetting.
They were not good or evil; they simply were.
Then came the Godstorm, a cataclysmic collision of divine forces that threatened mortal civilization entirely.

In the chaos, mortals ascended to godhood.
Lucifer was the first — a god of light, preservation, and order who carved out a bastion of civilization.
The New Gods displaced the Old, sealing their power in whiteglass prisons and ancient vaults.
But every suppression created an imbalance, and those imbalances compounded for centuries into a slow, invisible stagnation.

Then four adventurers, hired for a simple retrieval job, shattered the Seal of Unmaking and freed the God of Ruin.
Carrying the curse in their own bodies as the heralds of ruin, they raced its spread across the land and, alongside the Morrow family's secret worshippers of renewal, awakened the long-dormant God of Renewal beneath the Chryseum.
For one fragile moment the old balance held — but balance is not peace.
Ruin's passage left scars no ceremony could heal: a desiccated Ashen Vale, a collapsing beaver dam, ambitious lords filling the vacuum, and cults whispering of endings.
Along the Bonewall, the Thirteen Spines that have always guarded the world's edge have begun to go dark, and the Church's own records are quietly unraveling — names missing from liturgies unchanged for centuries.
Something is being forgotten at a scale larger than any single curse, and one of the heralds has vanished without a trace.
The ice that held everything in place is thawing, and the world is waking up.
