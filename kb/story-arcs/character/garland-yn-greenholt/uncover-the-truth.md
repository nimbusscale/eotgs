# Uncover the Truth

**Type:** Character ([[Garland yn Greenholt]])
**Theme:** Discover the Truth
**Status:** Active
**Started:** [[Session 1]]

## Summary
[[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]] chose "Uncover the Truth" as his character arc, driven by suspicion that his grandson [[Aldric Garlandsson|Aldric]] may be connected to the events at [[Ashbrook]].
The Aldric livery mystery has been resolved: the Harlequin Jimmy confessed that all the livery scraps were planted by the Harlequins as a trick to exploit Garland's paranoia, largely exonerating Aldric.
However, this only proves the livery was planted — not that Aldric is entirely uninvolved in broader events.
The full story behind the [[Seal of Unmaking]]'s retrieval and who truly benefits remains unknown.

## Open Questions
- Is there more to Aldric's role beyond what the Harlequins have revealed?
- Can the renewal energy fully restore the knowledge stolen by the forgetting curse?

## Answered Questions
- **Who was wearing Aldric's livery?** — The Harlequins planted it as a deliberate deception ([[Session 2]])
- **Is Aldric connected to the events at Ashbrook?** — The Harlequins fabricated the livery evidence, strongly suggesting he was not involved ([[Session 2]])
- **What is the full story behind the Seal of Unmaking's retrieval?** — The Morrow family are secret worshippers of renewal who deliberately hired the party to release ruin so that renewal could return; the Laughing One has corrupted the process ([[Session 4]])

## Key Events
- [[Session 1]] — Garland found Aldric's livery at Beaver Lake; Edric's investigation of Sergeant Iyer suggests Aldric is not directly involved; Garland avoided recognition in Crownvale but was spotted by Mira
- [[Session 2]] — Found another piece of Aldric's livery near [[The Nodrum]]; the Harlequin Jimmy confessed all the livery was planted as a trick; the Dark Harlequin sent Garland a disturbing vision of [[Mira]] and children at Aldric's castle
- [[Session 4]] — Challenged [[Luminary Severin Morrow|Severin]]'s claim to be a herald of renewal; his spellbook responded to residual renewal energy at the [[Shrine of Renewal]], partially counteracting the forgetting curse and offering the first real hope of recovering lost knowledge; confirmed his spellcraft originated from the [[Witch Stones]]
