# Curse of Ruin

**Type:** Group
**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active
**Started:** [[Session 0]]

## Summary
The party was hired by [[Count Albrecht Marrow]] to retrieve a twisted holy symbol from ruins in the mountains above the [[Ashen Vale]].
What should have been a straightforward job turned into something far worse.
The ruins — [[Ashbrook]], now called [[Eld Ashara]] — were not ancient; they had been a thriving settlement where [[Garland yn Greenholt]]'s great-grandchild [[Rowan yn Greenholt]] lived, now aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse.

The symbol itself was the [[Seal of Unmaking]], and by removing it, the party unwittingly released the God of Ruin it contained.
Now the curse spreads through the mountain springs, flowing downward toward the valley.
[[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]] erected a spirit wall at [[Beaver Lake]] to hold back the corruption, but the ruin is pooling at the barrier site and will eventually overwhelm it.
The beaver colony was evacuated downstream by [[Castor]].

The party realizes they were used — whoever hired [[Edric Bloom]] knew exactly what would happen.
They are no longer just adventurers; they are the only ones who can make things right.

At [[The Nodrum]], [[The Triune]] revealed that ruin had a counterpart — the God of Renewal — whose absence makes the current catastrophe so devastating.
Centuries of pent-up ruinous energy were released all at once.
The Triune identified the party as the **heralds of ruin** — carrying the curse wherever they go, not through the water.
Scrying confirmed the spirit wall at [[Beaver Lake]] blocks nothing; the ruin moved with the party.
Ruin targets each person's core identity: protection ([[Sir Roderic Lightbearer|Roderic]]), knowledge ([[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]]), inspiration ([[Edric Bloom|Edric]]), creation ([[Castor]]).

The Harlequins — followers of the God of Trickery — revealed they have been following the party since [[Ashbrook]], manipulating events for the Laughing One's entertainment.
The path forward requires finding the heralds of renewal; a shrine beneath the Chryseum in [[Aurelion]] is their best lead.

## Answered Questions
- **Who was wearing Aldric's livery?** — The Harlequin Jimmy confessed the scraps were planted by the Harlequins as a trick to exploit Garland's paranoia ([[Session 2]])
- **Is ruin spreading through the river?** — No; scrying showed no ruin at [[Beaver Lake]]; the corruption travels with the party themselves ([[Session 2]])
- **Can the spirit wall hold?** — The spirit wall still stands but blocks nothing; the ruin moved with the party ([[Session 2]])
- **Is Aldric connected to Ashbrook?** — The Harlequins fabricated the livery evidence, strongly suggesting he was not involved ([[Session 2]])
