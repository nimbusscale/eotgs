- Despite Castor technically being older (cursed as a beaver longer than Garland has been alive), Garland is the "elder" figure
- **Garland helped lift Castor's curse** — giving him back his humanity (whether Castor wanted it or not)
- **Key Goal:** Understand Castor's curse—not because Castor wants it undone, but so Garland knows how to undo it "just in case" before his own time runs out

**Primary Faith: The Luciferian Religion**

- Lucifer is the god of radiance/light
- The religion has genuinely good tenants — helping people, protecting the weak
- (We may come up with a better name for this religion)

- **Some places:** Mandated worship (Inquisition-style) — other religions forbidden

**Opposition:** A "God of Ruin" — associated with darkness, decay, and aging. An enemy/rival to Lucifer who was apparently banished at some point. (Name TBD)

**Other Faiths:** Smaller traditions exist (like Edric's private religion), generally tolerated in Roderic's kingdom but not in all places.

## World Geography (Established)

### Historical Periods

**The Old Empire (Ancient Past)**
- A vast empire that spanned the land (think Rome)
- Ruled by Lucifer — the god-emperor figure the Luciferians now worship
- The empire eventually fell, leading to a "dark ages" period

**The Middle Kingdoms (Post-Empire)**
- A confederacy of kingdoms and city-states that formed after the empire's fall
- Eventually this confederacy also fell apart
- **Edric's yellow scarf** bears the symbols of these Middle Kingdoms — the kings and kingdoms that made up this confederacy

**The Modern Kingdoms (Current Era)**
- More independent and disorganized than the Middle Kingdoms
- Each kingdom is essentially its capital city + immediate surroundings
- Some kingdoms have a main city and a few minor cities
- Outside the cities and main roads = **the wild**

### The Nature of "Civilization"
This is a **points of light** setting:
- Kingdoms may *claim* mountains, valleys, and regions, but they only truly *control*:
  - The capital/cities
  - Main roads
  - Inns along those roads
  - Checkpoints and fortresses
- Go a mile or two off the beaten path and you're in the wild
- Maps are not very good
- The roads are relatively secure; off-road is dangerous

**What's in the Wild:**
- Remnants of the Old Empire
- Ruins of the Middle Kingdoms
- People who don't fit civilization
- Monsters that have cropped up
- The unknown

### Specific Locations

**Roderic's Kingdom (Beaconhold):** The kingdom Roderic grew up in. The Luciferian faith is the **state religion** here — supported but not mandated. Other religions are tolerated but not supported (perhaps frowned upon).
- The **Ashen Vale** and **Eastern Rivers** are border/hinterland regions of this kingdom
- The capital city is where Roderic was raised
- Details to be fleshed out in play

**Other Political Entities:** Multiple other kingdoms and city-states exist in the setting. None have been established yet — these will be developed in play as political intrigue unfolds. (Ken and Ramsey are both interested in this element.)

**The Eastern Rivers:** Border/hinterland region on the eastern edge of the kingdom. Edric's homeland. Remote enough that tax collectors don't visit.

**The Ashen Vale:** A border region to the south of the kingdom, where Garland and Castor live. Associated with curse-related phenomena and home to the legendary Witch Stones (Shepherd's Teeth).

**The Cursed Ruins:** South of the kingdom, near the Ashen Vale. Originally was a functioning town that Garland had relatives in, but has been reduced to ruins by the aging curse. Sits atop a spring whose water now carries the curse downstream.

### Biography

Garland, just Garland, is a very old man entering his third act. He served as a soldier in his youth, marching with drum and fife in hand as a child, and retired to a barren plot of land earned as spoils of war.

With his wife long gone (and, in fact, several of his children having passed as well), and tired of being called lord, he left the farm in the hands of the ever-growing clan he begat, and set off with fife and drum in hand.

### Biography

I was born to a woman who could sing any room quiet, somewhere along the eastern rivers—though the river's name has changed at least twice since then. I never knew my father, but I apprenticed for the local merchant when I was a lad. He taught me how to listen for what people needed before they knew to ask, and my mother taught me how to give it to them with a story.

When I was a teen, I left that village, following markets and festivals, learning which songs opened doors and which ones closed them gently. Somewhere along the way I realized people would trade almost anything for the right tale at the right moment, and that stories travel better if you travel with them.

Since then I've made my living carrying things people want but can't easily explain—songs, curiosities, names of the powerful—and seeing where they take me next.

Edric Bloom is a lean man with a calm, steady presence and an Unmistakeable Voice that carries without effort. He dresses like someone who has traveled long roads and slept in good and bad rooms alike. Around his neck he wears a distinctive Faded Yellow Scarf, finely woven, its weave occasionally revealing a broken sunburst ring — an old royal symbol of a fallen line of kings — that only becomes visible when the fabric moves just right. He moves with a calm, steady grace and Never Seems Rushed, even when others expect him to be. It's the kind of thing that draws questions he never fully answers.

### Biography

Years and generations ago, Castor was a modest carpenter, respected for his craft but largely considered unremarkable by his people. He worked his trade while also tending a small homestead among the land shared by his family. It was a simple life but it worked for Castor and for his part he wanted only the simpler pleasures in life. He was never good with people and seemed uninterested in growing out his own branch of the family.

But everything changed when his ancestral land came under a curse... a curse that seemed to pick on him in particular\! *\[Still haven't decided on the means and motive of the curse\]* Whether it be bad luck or revenge for a forgotten affront, Castor in particular took the brunt of the curse far worse than his brethren — He was transformed into a beaver and left to wander the land.

For his part Castor took to his new life as well as could be expected. He grew to know his local beavers and actually found some satisfaction in a dam well built. Seemingly untouched by time in this form, he took to exploring the world nearby and found himself enamored with his new perspective and his fellow animals, even those that would want him as a meal.

But growing tired of his travels and curious as to what made him how he is, Castor returned to the cursed homelands that changed him. There he found an elderly descendant trying to make the most of the land. Castor did what he could to assist while also being a bit of a chaos gremlin from time to time. But with the help of some self taught magic, this descendant stumbled across an insight into the curse and that it had affected this humble beaver.

The descendant attempted to lift the curse, but ultimately it was only mitigated. Castor could once again assume his original form, but after so much time spent as a fuzzball he found himself more comfortable as a beaver. Still his loyalty to his family (who never seemed to bother remembering him) and his curiosity surrounding the curse that changed him has lead him to engage his wanderlust once more.
