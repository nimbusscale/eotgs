# Order of the Eclipsed Sword

**Type:** Organization
**Status:** Active

## Overview
The military arm of the Luciferian Inquisition ([[Corona Vigil]]).
They arrived at Crest Aurelion to arrest [[Count Albrecht Marrow]] and [[Luminary Severin Morrow]] for their heterodox beliefs about ruin and renewal.
Their arrival directly threatens the party's mission, as Count Marrow is the missing herald needed to complete the connection at the [[Shrine of Renewal]].

## Relationships
- [[Corona Vigil]] — Their parent organization; they serve as the Inquisition's military enforcement
- [[The Light]] — Part of the Church's enforcement apparatus
- [[Count Albrecht Marrow]] — Attempting to arrest him
- [[Luminary Severin Morrow]] — Attempting to arrest him
- [[Sir Roderic Lightbearer]] — Fellow Luciferian; his mission now conflicts with their orders
