# Grimwild Campaign Index

## Campaign Overview

The party was hired by Count Albrecht Marrow to retrieve a twisted holy symbol from the ruins of Ashbrook in the Cinderwall mountains. What should have been a straightforward job turned into catastrophe. The ruins were not ancient — they had been a thriving settlement where Garland's great-grandchild Rowan lived, now aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse. By removing the Seal of Unmaking from its altar, the party unwittingly released the God of Ruin it contained.

Now the curse spreads through the mountain springs, flowing toward the Ashen Vale. Garland erected a spirit wall at Beaver Lake to hold back the corruption, but it will not last forever. The party travels to Beacon Hold to seek help from the Church of Lucifer and the Wizard's College, while suspicion grows about who orchestrated their involvement — and what dark purposes lie behind it all.
