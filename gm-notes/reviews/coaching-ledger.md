# GM Coaching Ledger

Running, cross-session synthesis of the `/review-session` feedback — the through-line
that no single session review can show. Read at the **start** of every review (for
continuity) and updated at the **end** (with that session's deltas).

Scope is **Chasing Adventure / PbtA craft only** — running *this* system better,
session over session. Not general GMing, not table logistics.

---

## Recurring strengths

_Patterns of strong CA play that show up across multiple sessions. Cite the sessions._

- **Fictional positioning & intent** — asks "what's your goal?"/"what do you want to
  happen?" before locking a Move and lets the fiction pick the Defy stat instead of
  defaulting (S3: Garland enduring +STR; S4: the sword-heave correction at 1099-1135,
  Garland heaving +STR vs. +DEX; table adopted "describe the outcome, not the action").
- **Portray a Lively World** — NPCs and even animals pursue their own goals; ruin shown
  in down-to-earth detail (S3: faded Aurelion, Dorn's mended cloak, Tufa's rat society;
  S4: Brenn/Severin's own theology and agency, the Harlequin who picks his own lock and
  bolts, moss in the floor-cracks and torches shifting to white light).
- **Spotlight distribution** — _graduated from growth area in S4; held in S5; regressed on
  cadence in S6; cadence fix landed in S7; held in S8._ Explicit, frequent spotlight passes
  across a multi-PC scene and active pull-ins for the quiet player (S4: "moving the spotlight
  to Garland," checking in with Castor at 1027/2260). S5: under a full party split, the GM
  self-diagnosed Castor's S4 sidelining and gave him his own solo scene — Castor topped
  player mic time. **S6 caveat:** the three-thread split was run as **sequential ~30-min
  vignettes** rather than intercut, so non-active players disengaged (Roderic AFK through
  Paxton's scene, 951). **S7 fixed it:** the converging threads were intercut in short
  segments cut on beats (697, 1537, 1720), and GM mic share fell to **47.6%** — players
  out-talking the GM for the first time. **S8 held it** under a four-way split + a
  three-location intercut brawl (cuts on beats at 676, 863, 1060, 1323, 1607); players
  out-talked the GM a second straight session and the table explicitly endorsed the handling
  (2337-2344). Remaining edge is just holding the *quiet* PC (Castor) in his own beats —
  though S8 gave him the pivotal action (the gorilla grab) despite low mic time. (GM mic
  share 53.7% S4 → 63.7% S5 → 59.0% S6 → 47.6% S7 → 49.4% S8.)
- **Collaborative "Ask Them" worldbuilding** — _peaked in S6; new apex of player-authored
  *stakes* in S8._ Turning exposition into player-owned fiction via "how would you know?" and
  direct asks (S3: herald-impact prompt, signature-weapon ask, the Dorn-flashback vantage).
  S6 was almost wholly co-authored — Roderic's tomb backstory, the Reaches geography and
  barge, the beaver politics, Mira's portal. **S8** ran the entire memory-stone sequence as
  "we come up with what you see together" (92), let Garland narrate his own vision (236) and
  author the spirit-realm ritual's cost live (824-848) — culminating in the player *choosing*
  to sacrifice the memory of his son Dafydd (859). Co-authorship now reaches into irreversible
  personal stakes, not just lore. _Note: under-used in S4's long debate._
- **Leave Things Open / play to find out** — runs unprepared content and folds player
  improvisation into canon (S3: the mine route, Tufa improvised into a lasting arc;
  S4: herald identity left open, Garland's spellbook beat canonized; S6: nearly the entire
  session co-authored and adjusted on the fly).
- **Think Dangerously actually landing** — _strength, graduated in S6; held in S7 and S8._
  S5 let a man die irreversibly (but aimed at no PC); S6 landed a PC condition (Garland's
  tackle) *and* irreversible innocent deaths caused by a PC's own reckless choice (Paxton
  burning the barge). S7 held it (Paxton near-crumble, Roy dead, Mira's voice gone, the tomb
  collapse). **S8 held it and pointed it at a PC's emotional core:** Garland sacrifices the
  memory of his son Dafydd to save Mira (859); Roderic's sword is snapped by Loka (1543) and
  Roderic is thrown down the stairs (2115); the session cuts on tentacles breaking through the
  ceiling (2146). Costs are increasingly *player-authored* and irreversible.
- **Foregrounding the grim choice *before* it lands / 7-9 led with an ugly choice** —
  _new S7; confirmed S8._ The barge Make Them Choose (S7 211-216) is now joined by S8's Paxton
  Defy +DEX, resolved as a Make Them Choose (knock over a memory stone of unknown effect, or
  take the tackle — 1596-1604) — the exact S7 carry-forward, cashed. The warhorse-trample
  softness from S7 did not recur; S8's Engage 7-9s were also fiction-first (sword snapped 1543,
  Roderic thrown down the stairs 2115).

## Recurring growth areas

_System-specific habits to work on that recur across sessions. Cite the sessions,
and note whether each is trending better, worse, or flat._

- **Making Moves at non-roll trigger points — ignored threats / live drive-hooks**
  (S3, S4, S5, S6) — _resolved in S7, moving toward a strength._ S3/S4 the gap was
  *softening 6-s*; S5/S6 the edge moved to firing a Move *at* a PC when a threat is live and
  no dice are out, with one uncashed **social** threat in S6 (the demand for Castor). **S7
  closed it:** the ambush arrow + six men close on Roderic the instant his Scrutinize lands
  (420), the tomb collapses and drops him into the river (1456), the giant vine bats the
  Slate youth at Paxton (577), the spider erupts on his Unleash-Radiance cost (644). No
  uncashed live threat this session. The lone remaining softness migrated to a *roll*
  consequence (the trample 7-9, below), not a non-roll miss.
- **Think Dangerously — Hard/irreversible consequences** (S3, S4, S5, S6) — _resolved in S6,
  moving to a strength._ S4 had zero Hard Moves; S5 landed a death but aimed at no PC; **S6
  landed both a PC condition (Garland) and irreversible innocent deaths caused by a PC's own
  reckless choice (Paxton burning the barge with stowaways aboard).** The break is now
  pointed at a PC. Remaining refinement is *timing* — foreground such consequences as a
  *choice before* the PC commits, not a reveal afterward (the stowaway screams came after
  Paxton was already clear).
- **Pacing / Gloss Over — long stretches past their tension** (S4) — _improved in S5._
  The vault escape converted its stated clock (Marrow working the door while soldiers
  close) into an actual hold-the-line fight. S5's compression was deliberate (wrapping
  the prologue) and reasonable; the one un-spent clock was the mob breaking into the
  Chryseum above Castor.
- **State the threat before the roll** (S3) — _trending up, nearly resolved._ S4 front-
  loaded the threat on Castor's key-grab and self-corrected on Garland's sneak.
- **Consequence variety beyond conditions** (S3, S4) — _resolved S5, confirmed S6 → strength;
  localized dip in S8._ S6 widened it (PC condition, a **Favor debt** Garland→Iyer, abandoned
  equipment, collateral deaths). **S8** kept the range at the thread level (Garland's memory of
  Dafydd, Loka snapping Roderic's sword, a new Favor bargain to keep Loka alive, the Paxton
  Make-Them-Choose) — but the *upstairs brawl* flattened to a condition/armor default, which
  the GM noticed aloud ("doing a condition to you is kind of boring… trying to think of what
  else could be interesting," 1900) and then did anyway. Not a regression to S3/S4 levels;
  a single sub-fight where the next-tier Move (Take Something Away / Change the Environment)
  was on the tip of his tongue. Fix unchanged: on that kind of miss, drop the weapon, topple a
  petrified watcher, seal off the stair.
- **Encourage Exciting Risks in exposition-heavy scenes** (S5, S6, S7, S8) — _flat/recurring,
  the most stubborn open note._ When a thread is pure investigation, info is handed over with no
  in-the-moment jeopardy (S6 Beaconhold/Gregory; S7 the tavern militiaman; S8 the Vaalo/Hiillos
  lore-dump, 388-476). S8's one good instinct: the Hiillos reveal points toward a dangerous
  objective ("it's very dangerous, the stones there have shifted," 423). Fix: frame a piece of
  the lore as a *ticking* risk before handing it over — e.g. Foreshadow that the children of
  Hiillos are dimming faster than ever, "you would have to reach the cradle before the next one
  goes dark." Four sessions flat now.

- **Make a Move when a social scene stops evolving** (S8) — _new._ The Loka theological
  standoff cycled "Moldrex lies / no, we are certain" across two long stretches (886-1055,
  1357-1476) without the situation changing — the GM self-diagnosed it (2350-2367). A PbtA
  scene that stops producing new fiction is the cue to make a Move. Fix: cut the loop with a
  **Make Them Choose** built from the scene's stakes ("the warriors slip past with the child
  toward the Suonet *while* you argue — keep working on Loka, or break off and chase them?")
  or **Set Up an Immediate Danger** off Loka himself.
- **Leading a 7-9 Defy with a real ugly choice, not a "worse outcome" reveal** (S7) — _new,
  one regression._ S6 nailed it (Paxton's drop-the-sword-or-stay) and S7's barge Make Them
  Choose was textbook — but Roderic's 8 on the warhorse trample resolved as a flat reveal
  (more men ahead + you notice the necklaces, 482-484), no sacrifice, with the GM flagging
  his own unease (475). It also skipped a Paladin Mercy test Garland handed him (trample the
  man wearing a holy symbol, 467). Fix: lead the 7-9 with the choice using the man already
  down — "hooves through him and you're clear, or rein up and they drag you off the saddle."
- **Re-anchoring Castor's ill-fitting drive** (S3, S4, S5, S6, S7, S8) — _the single most
  stubborn growth area; now diagnosed._ Castor can't claim his drive at End of Session yet
  again (S6 1009/1024; S7 2333; S8 2282-2293), thinnest mic every time (5.3% S6 → 7.4% S7 →
  7.5% S8). **S8 is the turning point in understanding it:** the GM did *exactly* the S7 rec —
  the Dark Harlequin tempt pointed Castor at the child (763) and he risked himself as a gorilla
  to save Itu (1273) — and the drive *still* didn't fire, because the table then argued the
  child isn't an "outcast." The GM's setup is now correct; **the drive text is the mismatch.**
  Fix shifts from "point him at an outcast" (done) to **rewrite the drive with the player** to
  match the arc actually being played: spectator → one who acts / reclaiming his lost humanity
  (a drive claimable on the gorilla leap and the memory-stone grief alike).
- ~~Spotlight distribution in long solo scenes~~ (S3) — **resolved in S4**, moved to
  Recurring strengths.
