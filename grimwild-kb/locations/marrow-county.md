# Marrow County

**Type:** Region
**First Visited:** [[Session 0]]

## Description
A minor but strategically placed county between [[Beaconhold]] county and the [[Ashen Vale]].
Ruled by [[Count Albrecht Marrow]].
Deeply entwined with the [[Church of Lucifer]].

## Notable Features
- Strategically positioned between the capital and the Ashen Vale
- The Count is a major donor to the Church, with several sons in influential clerical positions

## Connected Locations
- [[Beaconhold]] — The kingdom and capital county it borders
- [[Ashen Vale]] — The region to the south

## Associated NPCs
- [[Count Albrecht Marrow]] — Ruler of Marrow County

## Events Here
