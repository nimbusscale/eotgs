# Rowan yn Greenholt

**First Appeared:** [[Session 0]]
**Status:** Deceased (presumed)
**Affiliation:** Independent

## Description
Great-grandchild of [[Garland yn Greenholt]].
Full name: Rowan yn Greenholt ap Hawkworth.
She and her partner Corin settled in [[Ashbrook]] before the town fell to the curse.

## Role
Her final missive to Garland — addressed to "Da-Gar" — contained several ominous signs that were not recognized at the time.
She mentioned a stranger carrying a wrapped object who wore what appeared to be cousin Aldric's livery.
She noted the stonework near the spring changing, the water tasting unusually sweet, and fish not lingering in the shallows.
She mentioned talk of renaming the town to something grander.

She perished when [[Ashbrook]] fell to the curse and became [[Eld Ashara]].

## Relationships
- [[Garland yn Greenholt]] — Her great-grandfather, whom she called "Da-Gar"
- [[Aldric Garlandsson]] — Cousin (she spotted his livery on a stranger visiting Ashbrook)

## Key Events
- [[Session 0]] — Her letter is part of the backstory; she perished when Ashbrook fell

## Notes
Her letter is a key piece of evidence connecting events before the curse struck Ashbrook.
