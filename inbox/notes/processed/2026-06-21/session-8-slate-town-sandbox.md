# Session 8 — The Slate Town (Sandbox)

> **GM ONLY.** A live-play sandbox, not a plot outline. Everything below is a menu of pressure points and drama to draw on as the table reveals what it cares about. New names and connections are **proposed** (mark = ▶) so you can accept, cut, or merge on the fly. Established canon is **locked** (mark = ●).

## 1. The shape in one breath

The party is resting amid the Slate's celebration of the first child. They settle in, bond, and learn the town across a night. The next day, a faction **already inside the town** moves to seize the child — not raiders from outside, but Slate of the town itself, recruited by an offer from beyond the wall. The choice the party makes here either heads off Moldrex's first foothold among the Slate or solidifies it.

The whole thing rhymes: **real renewal and counterfeit renewal arrive at the same moment.** The Awakening gives the Slate a true child; Moldrex offers them a way to stop needing the mountain at all.

## 2. New canon to capture (for the KB)

▶ **Mira is a cleric of the Witch Stones.** The Stones are her deity — a font of power tied to an as-yet-unknown emanation (candidate: the Old God of memory hypothesis; possibly kin to the Slate's "stone god" / Syvämaa's buried sun — leave open).

The creed, as Jay framed it:

- **Domain:** the earth, and the living things that crawl and grow upon it. *("The land abides.")*
- **Aspect:** endurance, inevitability, remembrance. *("The land endures.")*
- **Worshippers:** the lost line of the Greenholt blood once kept the faith; Mira's calling is to bring it back to the people. *("The land provides.")*
- **Enemies:** despoilers of the past; the rootless, and the revelers in the ephemeral. *("The land remembers.")*
- **Demands:** preserve what must endure; leave the world stronger for those who come after. *("The land survives.")*

**Why this matters this session:** the Slate are endurance, inevitability, and remembrance made flesh — a whole people built around the thing Mira is becoming. And the rival faction, by reaching for Moldrex's ephemeral transformation, becomes the literal doctrinal **enemy** of her faith: the rootless, the revelers in the ephemeral, the despoilers of what must endure. Mira's god and these people are the same force; the rivals are heresy against it. This is the first time her faith meets its opposite in the world. *(Don't have her articulate all this — let it press on her.)*

## 3. The cast

Names are Finnish-grounded to match Vaalo / Kehto / Hiillos / Syvämaa. The party hears them through Mira's translation, so each carries an epithet she can render in plain speech. All ▶ proposed.

● **The Slate have no gender.** Mountain-born, no biological reproduction, no reason for sex — androgynous humanoid beings. Every Slate below is **they**.

### The child
**▶ Itu** ("the Seed," *EE-too*) — the first to emerge in an age. ● Established as the first child; ▶ the twist below makes it the first to *survive*. Play it small, curious, and precious. It should become real to at least one PC during the settle-in.

### Friendly faction (the town as it stands)

**▶ Routa** ("Deep-Frost," *ROW-tah*) — eldest guardian, raised Itu, embodies the whole Stagnation survived. Slow, warm, immovable in the good sense. The emotional anchor: if the party bonds with Routa, the child's danger lands as personal stakes, not abstraction.

**▶ Kallio** ("Bedrock," *KAL-lee-oh*) — stone-scribe elder who tends the Bonewall witch-ring and reads the old carvings. **Garland & Mira's contact.** Holds the people's stone-memory but cannot work the ring as a mage; needs what Mira hears.

**▶ Holvi** ("the Vault," *HOL-vee*) — loremaster elder who still holds **Vaalo's name** and the truth of the Spines. **Roderic & Paxton's contact.** *Mergeable into Kallio on the fly if the table has too many elders — keep Holvi in your back pocket as you said.*

### Rival faction (embedded inside the town)

**▶ Aamu** ("the Dawnkeeper," *AH-moo*) — **Keeper of Emergence**, the one who presides over the mountain's births. Faction **leader**, and the sympathetic core. Carries the secret (§4). Reachable, because the wound is grief, not zeal. Aamu has the standing to redefine what the Slate are; that's why the warrior needs them.

**▶ Lohka** ("the Boulder," *LOH-kah*) — **head warrior**, nearing petrification. The combat foil. Not despairing — *wanting*. Wants Moldrex's gift of never returning to the stone. Cannot lead this movement alone (no cachet), so they back Aamu. Talk Aamu down and you don't stop Lohka — you *trigger* them, because their only road to not-dying just walked away.

**▶ Sora** ("Gravel," *SOH-rah*) — Lohka's lieutenant, younger and fanatically loyal. Gives the fight a second voice and a second body so it isn't a single target.

**Younger loyal warriors** — unnamed combat body. Visibly **devolving** (see §4).

## 4. The secret and the offer

### The secret (the diplomatic lever)
▶ **Itu is not the first to emerge. Itu is the first to survive.** Others came before, stillborn — and **the town does not know. Only Aamu knows** (locked — not even Routa). Aamu has hidden the failures, out of **mercy, not coercion** (Moldrex is not holding it over them). They are protecting the people from despair.

The tragedy: the same protective silence that shields the town is what isolates Aamu enough for Moldrex's offer to take root. No one knows the weight they carry, so no one can talk them out of it.

The lever: if the party uncovers this, the move isn't "argue Moldrex is bad." It's **bring the grief into the open. Let Aamu not be alone with it.** That competes with Moldrex on the actual wound. (Mira's creed cuts here too: *the land remembers* — the faith that holds grief and endurance is the answer to the keeper who's been carrying both alone.)

### The offer (Moldrex)
Not a threat — a seduction, and a complete one. Framed as a **divorce from the Old Gods**: the Slate are a people *of* the abiding order; Moldrex offers them out of it.

- **Control over birth** (Aamu's want): make your own children, like the surface folk — never again at the mercy of a mountain that keeps losing them.
- **Control over death** (Lohka's want): never return to the stone. Petrification stops being destiny.

Both ends of the lifecycle, severed from the mountain. *The land endures / the land survives* — Mira's god — is exactly what Moldrex is selling them an escape from.

### The devolution (what the change actually is)
The Slate's nature is to *harden* toward permanence — stone moving toward stillness. Moldrex's "evolution" is the exact inversion: it drags them **backward into soft biological life.** Under the cracking stone exterior is real flesh — they are devolving from enduring stone beings into perishable creatures.

This is thematically the whole point. What Moldrex sells as ascension is a **fall into the ephemeral**, and it reads directly against Mira's creed: the rivals are literally trading endurance and remembrance for the perishable. They become *the revelers in the ephemeral* in the most literal sense.

**Mechanical/visual consequence:** the exposed flesh under broken stone is a **weakness the party can see and target** — a soft seam in what used to be armor. The more transformed a warrior, the more flesh shows, the more vulnerable they are. A grim irony to surface in the fight: the "stronger, freer" Slate are the easiest to wound.

▶ Suggested staging: **Aamu not visibly changed** (still deciding, with their grief — keeps them talkable and sympathetic). **Lohka and the loyal warriors** showing it — stone split at the joints, flesh beneath. Reads against Castor's curse (§7: forced devolution vs. their chosen one).

> Note: the **mutated vines are their own Bonewall phenomenon**, not part of the warriors' transformation. Keep them separate (see §8 for the optional surface front).

## 5. Locations

Eight places, each tagged to what it earns at the **End of Session** (▣ Discover · ▣ Visit/Alter · ▣ Overcome) and to whose **drive** it feeds, so every XP axis has a home. Sensory blocks are kept in house style for Claude Code / KB lift-out. Matches the cutaway map spec (`map-slate-town-cutaway`).

### Surface — The Bonewall Ridge
The zoomed surface section: dead grey shale, one side of the wall only (Öuth Krelt off-frame).

**▶ The Bonewall Witch-Ring**
- *Sight:* a rough ring of standing stones on open shale, twin to Garland's own but carved in the unreadable runic tongue; worn nearly silent.
- *Sound:* a low, almost-subsonic hum; the wind; faint grinding when Mira channels.
- *Smell:* cold stone, dry lichen, ozone after a channeling.
- **Earns:** ▣ Discover (the ring's secrets) · ▣ Overcome (the vine attack, §8). **Drive hook:** *Garland — "Step forward where others hesitate"* (be first into the vine fight or the channeling risk) · Mira's attunement plays here (no Drive; NPC).

**▶ The Vine Cave-Mouth** ● *(established entry — Paxton, S7)*
- *Sight:* a low cave in the slope, choked with oversized discolored grey vines; fresh scorch marks where Paxton burned a path.
- *Sound:* creak of growing wood; drip; something shifting in the green dark.
- *Smell:* wet rot, char, a sweetish wrongness under it.
- **Earns:** ▣ Overcome. **Drive hook:** *Paxton — "Act callously or recklessly about death"* (the place he already wrenched a shoulder and nearly died saving a young Slate; ripe for him to spend himself again) · *Castor — "for wildlife or outcasts"* (the vines, the wild gone wrong).

### The Descent — Entrances into the Mountain
**▶ The Witch-Ring Stair** — worn fissure dropping from the ring into the cutaway; the second on-map entrance. Garland & Mira's way down.
**▶ The River-Collapse Entry** ● — Roderic's fall (S7); **off-frame**, far south by the tomb, reached now only by the south speedway.

### Interior

**▶ The Hall of the Settled** *(town proper)*
- *Sight:* a great pillared hall where every column and arch is a petrified ancestor merged into the stone; the celebration of Itu still glowing in lit hollows.
- *Sound:* the grinding stone-tongue; a slow, deep communal sound like the mountain breathing.
- *Smell:* mineral dust, cold water, warm rock near the lit hollows.
- **Earns:** ▣ Visit/Alter (the party's standing with the town is set or broken here). **Drive hook:** *Roderic — "save or protect someone"* and *Castor — "for outcasts"* both seed here through bonding with Routa and Itu; the hub where later protection-Drives get their stakes.

**▶ The Cradle-Hollow** *(birthing chamber — Aamu's domain)*
- *Sight:* a rounded, womb-like hollow where the rock turns soft and almost organic; a warm glow; Itu, newly emerged.
- *Sound:* a heartbeat-slow pulse in the stone; near-silence otherwise.
- *Smell:* warm wet mineral, like rain on hot rock.
- **Earns:** ▣ Discover (how emergence works; the cycle restarting). **Drive hook:** *Castor — "Endanger yourself for wildlife or outcasts"* (Itu and the Slate as outcasts; the renewal he's tied to) · *Roderic — "save or protect someone"* (the child as the thing to guard).

**▶ The Gallery of the Lost** *(the secret)*
- *Sight:* a quiet side-niche off the Cradle-Hollow; a row of small, still, unfinished forms in low niches. Easy to miss.
- *Sound:* nothing. The one silent place.
- *Smell:* cold, dry, settled.
- **Earns:** ▣ Discover (the stillbirth secret — the diplomatic lever, §4). **Drive hook:** not a Drive site so much as the lever that turns Aamu; Mira's faith (*the land remembers*) resonates but earns her nothing mechanically (NPC).
- ⚠️ GM-only. Render and reveal subtly; this is the engine of the whole faction conflict.

**▶ The Hall of Deep Memory** *(Holvi's domain)*
- *Sight:* walls dense with glyph-carved memory-stones in the Slate's runic script; the oldest, most weathered ancestor-figures stand here.
- *Sound:* a deeper grinding; the stones here "speak" longest.
- *Smell:* age, dry dust, faint metal.
- **Earns:** ▣ Discover (Vaalo's name; the Spines' true origin; Hiillos as banked ember). **Drive hook:** not a Drive trigger itself, but the discovery that *redirects* Roderic's and Paxton's protect/recklessness Drives toward Kehto and Carnforth next session.

### The Network & The Deep

**▶ The Speedways** *(new canon ▶)*
- *Sight:* smooth, ancient, glyph-carved conduits, unmistakably built and purposeful, branching off the rough caves; two run prominently — **north to Carnforth**, **south to Kehto** — others sealed or dormant.
- *Concept:* the Slate's fast-travel network linking fixed nodes along the wall. Fast travel between key points, **not** free roaming. Explains their tie to the tomb and the towers.
- **Earns:** ▣ Discover. **Drive hook:** logistics more than Drive — a fast road to the tomb and the failing Spines; sets up next session's protect/recklessness Drives at Carnforth.

**▶ The Descending Stair** *(Syvämaa hint)*
- *Sight:* at the lowest point, a great stair vanishing into a deep amber glow rising from far below; half-sealed, unexplored.
- *Sound:* a faint warmth-hum, felt more than heard.
- *Smell:* warm air rising, unlike anything above.
- **Earns:** ▣ Discover (a future road). **Drive hook:** none yet; plants the descent to the Deep-earth (Castor's curiosity, Mira's faith, the stone-god question). Do not open it this session.

## 6. Soft structure (beats, not rails)

A loose clock. Players can move through fast, slow, or sideways.

**Beat 1 — Settle & Bond (the night).** Celebration continues. Roleplay menu open (§7). Itu becomes real to someone. The rivals are present but reading as ordinary townsfolk — plant Lohka and Aamu in scenes now so the next-day turn is a betrayal, not a stranger.

**Beat 2 — The Crack (where the secret can surface).** A wrong note: a too-careful elder, a sealed gallery of the dead, Mira hearing the stones grieve, Castor's bond sensing something *off* under the town's joy. The party can pull this thread or not.

**Beat 3 — The Move (next day).** Aamu's faction acts to take Itu. Open it with an **ultimatum, not violence** — gives diplomacy a door. The party's standing with Routa and the town is suddenly in jeopardy: fight the rival Slate in front of the Slate they bonded with and they risk looking like the invaders.

**Beat 4 — The Choice & its cost.** Forks in §8–9.

**Moldrex clock (if players don't engage):** unattended, Aamu accepts the offer, Lohka's faction grows, Itu is taken beyond the wall, and Moldrex has his first foothold and his first willing Slate. The wall's wards weaken a little further. This is the *cost of doing nothing*, available to apply off-screen.

## 7. Interaction points by PC

### Garland & Mira — the ring and the faith
● Garland wants to read the Bonewall ring and reproduce Mira's gate; ● he can't read its runes; ● Mira hears the stones as words. **▶ Kallio** holds the stone-memory but no mage-craft.

▶ **Where each spends the downtime is open** (Jay's call on what Garland wants). Two plausible pulls: Garland to the ring with Kallio, Mira to the child below. If they're together, the three-way scene plays — the centuries-old wizard and the stone-elder both circling a ring neither can crack, and the **ten-year-old casually relays what it says**, the adults deferring to the child (pays off the established irony: the man relearning his lost mastery, out-paced by his apprentice). If they split, you get the two-front option in §8 and Mira's own thread with Itu.
- ▶ Mira's deeper beat: she meets a whole people of stone-memory — a living mirror of her faith — *and* meets its heresy in the rival faction. First real test of her calling. Her pull toward Itu (the thing her god provides) is a clean reason to keep her below with the child.
- ⚠️ Keep straight: Garland lost only his single most recent memory at the Nodrum (the note Mira carried), **not** his knowledge of her. No amnesia-about-Mira beats.

### Roderic & Paxton — Vaalo and the Light
Both are bound to the surface Light that **absorbed Vaalo's fire and forgot where it came from.** ● Roderic's company died in Vaalo's tomb seeking the lantern; ● Paxton is Light-made. **▶ Holvi** (or Kallio, merged) still holds the truth the Church forgot:
- The Spinewright was no son of the Imperium. His name was **Vaalo** (● GM-only until surfaced — most likely from the Slate; this is the place).
- The relic is **Hiillos**, a banked coal of an inner sun that burns *below*, not above.
- The tomb is **Kehto, the Cradle** — a return, not a grave.
- ▶ Gut-punch for Roderic: the massacre, the lost men, the lantern — reframed by a people who knew the truth all along. ▶ For Paxton: a thread into the deeper nature of his own Light. **Plants the return to Kehto with new eyes** (and recolors Carnforth's cold ember — the ward itself died, not just a flame).

### Castor — forced vs. chosen
Light touch. Castor watches Slate *willingly* choose a devolution into perishable flesh — the same kind of unwanted transformation that was *forced* on him. The mirror is sharper now: they're choosing to become softer, lesser, mortal, which is exactly what his curse did to him against his will. A quiet provocation, no backstory committed.
- ⚠️ Per your note: don't over-connect Castor to the **forgetting** theme (Garland and Mira own that). This is the *unnatural-evolution / devolution* mirror, which is fair game.
- ▶ Optional: Castor senses the wrongness under the town's joy through his bond as one-with-the-world / bridge between worlds — the same sense that felt the mountain's goodwill in S7 now catching a discordant note beneath it (a Beat-2 crack).

## 8. The combat (chosen, not forced)

The fight is **the warrior's refusal to die, made physical** — it happens because Lohka chooses it, not because the plot needed a brawl. Roderic gets his real encounter.

**Trigger:** the party talks **Aamu** down (via the secret, §4). Her stepping back removes Lohka's only road to immortality. **Lohka seizes the moment by force** and tries to take both Itu and the movement.

**The encounter (below, in the town):**
- **Lohka** — head warrior, visibly devolving, a genuine challenge. Soft flesh under split stone is the seam to target (§4).
- **Sora** — lieutenant, second body/voice.
- **Younger loyal warriors** — the body of the fight, devolving to varying degrees.

The grim irony to surface: the more "freed" by Moldrex, the more flesh shows, the easier to wound. Endurance was their armor; they traded it away.

**▶ Optional two-front staging.** If Garland and Kallio are up at the ring during the downtime, run the surface and the depths at once:
- **Surface (the ring):** the **mutated Bonewall vines** — ● their own phenomenon, separate from the warriors — attack Garland and Kallio at the witch-ring. A contained skirmish up top.
- **Below (the town):** the Slate faction fight — Lohka, Sora, the loyal warriors — around Itu.
- **Reunion:** the two fronts converge for the finish. Gives you parallel action and a satisfying join-up.
- *Collapse to a single front if timing's tight; the vines stay an available Bonewall threat regardless.*

**Texture to keep the fork alive:** even mid-fight, Lohka's faction can be individually talked down — but Lohka, with the stone closing on them, will not. A clean win means going through Lohka.

## 9. Outcome branches & world consequences

**A. Pure combat / kill them all.** Fast, satisfying for Ramsey, but the diplomatic door shuts: Aamu is never reached, the secret stays buried, and the *idea* survives even if these warriors don't. ▶ Moldrex's alliance with the rival impulse **solidifies** — the next Slate to despair has nowhere to turn but the wall. First foothold gained.

**B. Talk Aamu down, fight Lohka.** The strong tragic beat: Aamu turns, Lohka fights anyway, and **Aamu watches the people she recruited die for the offer she just refused.** Her grief doubles. The town learns the secret. Moldrex's foothold is **headed off** here — for now.

**C. Save / lose Itu.** Cross-cuts A and B. Losing Itu beyond the wall is the Moldrex-clock outcome and a long-running wound. Saving Itu cements standing with Routa and the town.

**D. The keeper after.** If Aamu lives and turns, she becomes a standing NPC carrying open grief — a potential ally, a potential relapse, and a living reminder that the town's renewal is fragile.

Whatever the mix: this session decides whether **Moldrex gains his first willing foothold among the Slate**, the through-line you wanted weaving the arc.

## 11. End-of-Session move — landing the XP

The goal: by session's end, every "yes" is earnable and every PC has had a real shot at their Drive, so XP is delivered honestly rather than fudged. The three group questions plus each Drive, with the in-session opportunities that satisfy them.

### The three group questions (1 XP each, whole party)

**▣ "Did we discover something new and important about the world or characters?"**
Hard to *miss* this session — the trick is making sure a discovery actually surfaces rather than staying in your notes. Reliable triggers: the stillbirth secret (Gallery of the Lost), Vaalo's name and the truth of the Spines (Hall of Deep Memory), how emergence works (Cradle-Hollow), the speedway network, or the Moldrex offer itself. *Insurance:* if the table somehow skips the deep lore, the offer and the secret both land just by engaging the faction conflict.

**▣ "Did we newly visit or significantly alter a memorable location?"**
⚠️ The party already claimed the *newly visit* XP for the Slate Town in S7 — so arriving/being here does **not** re-earn it. This question rests entirely on **significantly alter** this session, or on reaching a *new* location. Alter on-ramps: the town's standing toward outsiders shifts permanently (saved or lost child, secret exposed, warriors dead or turned); the Cradle-Hollow, Gallery, or Deep-Memory hall is changed by what happens there. New-location on-ramps: first descent down the **Speedways** or to the **Descending Stair** depths would count as newly visiting. Don't let the table claim this one for free on arrival.

**▣ "Did we overcome a notable obstacle, enemy, or challenge?"**
The Lohka fight is the obvious one, but keep it broad: talking Aamu down is *also* overcoming a challenge, as is the vine skirmish at the ring, or getting through the speedway/depths. A pure-diplomacy table still earns this by defusing the faction without a blade.

### Each PC's Drive (1 XP each, individual)

> Verified against the PC entries — these are the documented mechanical Drives, not vibes. Each needs a concrete on-ramp this session.

**Roderic — *"Endanger yourself to save or protect someone."***
The session is built for him. On-ramps: shield Itu when Lohka's faction moves; put himself between a Slate noncombatant and harm; hold the line at the Cradle-Hollow. Make sure at least one protect-able target is in danger *in front of him* during the raid.

**Paxton — *"Act callously or recklessly about death."***
Trickier and more interesting, because S7 had him *learning* not to spend lives cheaply. Give him a temptation, not a gimme: a moment where recklessness with his own death (or someone else's) is the fast path — burning a way through, throwing himself at Lohka, sacrificing a position to win. He earns the Drive whether or not it's the *wise* call; the tension is the point. Don't punish, just present the door.

**Garland — *"Step forward where others hesitate."***
On-ramps: be first into the vine fight at the ring; take the channeling risk at the witch-stones when no one else can; step up to broker with Aamu when the table stalls. Any hesitation-moment where Garland acts first delivers it. The two-front staging (if it happens) hands this to him naturally on the surface.

**Castor — *"Endanger yourself for wildlife or outcasts."***
The Slate *are* outcasts, and Itu is the ultimate vulnerable one. On-ramps: risk himself for the child, for a devolving warrior he pities, or for the young Slate from S7; use his world-bond to shield or rescue. His Beat-2 "something is wrong" sense is a clean lead-in to him sticking his neck out.

**Mira — no Drive (NPC).** She earns no XP; her role is translator, decoder, and emotional thread. Don't engineer XP beats for her.

### Quick GM checklist (glance mid-session)
- [ ] A discovery has actually *surfaced* out loud (not just available).
- [ ] The town has been *altered* (S7 already covered "visited"), **or** a genuinely new spot reached.
- [ ] A challenge has been *overcome* (fight **or** diplomacy counts).
- [ ] Roderic has had someone to protect *in danger*.
- [ ] Paxton has been *offered* a reckless door.
- [ ] Garland has hit a *hesitation moment* to step into.
- [ ] Castor has had an *outcast/wild* thing worth risking himself for.

*If a box is empty heading into the back half, that's your cue for what to put in front of the table next.*
