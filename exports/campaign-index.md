# Grimwild - Campaign Index

## Campaign Overview

The party was hired by Count Albrecht Marrow to retrieve a twisted holy symbol from ruins in the mountains above the Ashen Vale.
What should have been a straightforward job turned into something far worse.
The ruins were not ancient; they had been a thriving settlement called Ashbrook where Garland's great-grandchild Rowan lived, now aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse.

The symbol itself was the Seal of Unmaking, and by removing it, the party unwittingly released the God of Ruin it contained.
At the Nodrum, the Triune revealed that the party themselves are the heralds of ruin — carrying the curse wherever they go, not through the water.
Ruin targets each person's core identity: protection (Roderic), knowledge (Garland), inspiration (Edric), creation (Castor).

The Triune also revealed that ruin had a counterpart — the God of Renewal — whose absence makes the current catastrophe so devastating.
The Harlequins, followers of the God of Trickery, have been following the party since Ashbrook, manipulating events for the Laughing One's entertainment.
The path forward requires finding the heralds of renewal; a shrine to the God of Renewal beneath the Chryseum in Aurelion is their best lead.

The party is now wanted in Aurelion — posters accuse them of subverting the county and stealing goods.
They entered the city covertly through old mine tunnels beneath Aurelion, fought through rock rat ambushes and Aureate guards, and have breached past a critical junction leading toward the Chryseum district.

**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active

The ruins — Ashbrook, now called Eld Ashara — were not ancient; they had been a thriving settlement where Garland's great-grandchild Rowan lived, now aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse.
The symbol itself was the Seal of Unmaking, and by removing it, the party unwittingly released the God of Ruin it contained.
At the Nodrum, the Triune revealed that ruin had a counterpart — the God of Renewal — whose absence makes the current catastrophe so devastating.
Centuries of pent-up ruinous energy were released all at once.
The Triune identified the party as the heralds of ruin — carrying the curse wherever they go, not through the water.

### Character: Sir Roderic Lightbearer - Redemption
**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active

Roderic's crisis of faith deepened during the race to save the Ashen Vale.
His attempt to heal the exhausted horses at Beaver Lake was overwhelmed by ruin's proximity — the horses withered and aged before his eyes.
He interpreted this as confirmation that his faith is insufficient, the same weakness he blames for his battalion's destruction.
He is on assignment from the Church of Lucifer as a knight errant of "The Redeemers of Light," specifically tasked with redemption through deeds.
His successful blessings in the Crownvale tavern and his commanding invocation of Luciferian authority against the Aureate offer a counterpoint: his faith can still move people and compel mortal forces, even if it cannot yet overcome the old gods' power.
Ruin struck at Roderic's core identity as a protector — his door lock crumbled in his hands, and ley line energy passing through him emerged corrupted.

**Open Questions:**
- Can Roderic's faith overcome the influence of ruin, or is Lucifer's power insufficient against the old gods?
- What happened to Roderic's battalion, and was his faith truly to blame?
- Will the shrine of Renewal beneath the Chryseum challenge or strengthen his faith?
- Is there a conspiracy between Severin and Marrow?
