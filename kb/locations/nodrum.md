# The Nodrum

**Type:** Landmark
**First Visited:** [[Session 2]]

## Description
An ancient stone facility built into the side of a mountain at the nexus of several ley lines, deep in [[Marrow County]], not far from [[Aurelion]].
Once known as the Nexarium (or Aurelion Vault), it was originally a magical prison constructed during the wars of the [[Old Gods]] to contain [[The Xan-Kor]], a construct of the God of Forgetting.
The facade bears geometric glyphs of immense age, overlaid with Luciferian architectural stonework that has since worn away.

## Notable Features
- [[The Triune]] have retrofitted it with halfling-sized structures and equipment, including rotating platforms, mirrors, and lenses for their geometric magic
- Clockwork constructs serve as workers and guardians; clockwork spiders maintain the machinery
- Contains an antechamber library with history books documenting its origins as the Nexarium
- A central chamber with a deep pit where [[The Xan-Kor]] is held
- The door has been rebuilt at a middle size — large for halflings but tight for humans
- Sits at the nexus of several ley lines, making it a site of immense magical power
