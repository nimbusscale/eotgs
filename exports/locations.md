# Locations


## Regions


### Ashen Vale

**Type:** Region

A fertile valley enriched by volcanic ash from the [[Cinderwall]].
An independent region bordering the Kingdom of [[Beaconhold]].
Home to [[Garland yn Greenholt]] and [[Castor]].

**Notable Features:**
- Location of the [[Witch Stones]] (also called the Shepherd's Teeth), though their exact location is considered "lost" by locals
- Fed by the [[Ashen Flow|Lower Ashen Flow]], which runs clean after being filtered by the great beaver dam upstream
- Fertile farmland enriched by volcanic ash

**Connected Locations:** [[Beaconhold]], [[Crownvale]], [[Cinderwall]], [[Ashbrook]], [[Ashen Flow]]


### Marrow County

**Type:** Region

A minor but strategically placed county between [[Beaconhold]] county and the [[Ashen Vale]].
Ruled by [[Count Albrecht Marrow]].
Deeply entwined with the [[The Light]].
Its capital is [[Aurelion]].

**Notable Features:**
- Strategically positioned between the capital and the Ashen Vale
- The Count is a major donor to the Church, with several sons in influential clerical positions

**Connected Locations:** [[Aurelion]], [[Beaconhold]], [[Ashen Vale]]


### Eastern Rivers

**Type:** Region

An independent region bordering the Kingdom of [[Beaconhold]].
Fed by springs and snowmelt from the [[Bonewall]].
Remote enough that tax collectors don't visit.
The rivers cut through the [[Cragmarr]], providing passage to the [[Great Lake]].

**Notable Features:**
- [[Edric Bloom]]'s homeland
- River names have changed at least twice since Edric's birth

**Connected Locations:** [[Beaconhold]], [[Bonewall]], [[Cragmarr]], [[Great Lake]]


### The Cragmarr

**Type:** Region (Wilderness)

An independent region of cliffs, ravines, and treacherous terrain.
Lies between [[Beaconhold]]'s environs and the [[Great Lake]].
A labyrinth of broken rock — confusing and dangerous to navigate.
The [[Eastern Rivers]] cut through, providing the only reliable passage.

**Connected Locations:** [[Beaconhold]], [[Great Lake]], [[Eastern Rivers]]


## Mountain Ranges


### The Cinderwall

**Type:** Region (Mountain Range)

The southern mountain range forming part of the L-shaped border.
Wet and cold climate with volcanic activity that is the source of the [[Ashen Vale]]'s fertile ash.
Wooded slopes, mossy, shrouded in mist and steam.

**Notable Features:**
- Volcanic; source of the Ashen Vale's fertile ash
- Home to [[Dire Coyotes]] — a tougher and more aggressive breed that dominates the lower elevations toward the Ashen Vale
- Contains [[Ashbrook]] (now the cursed ruins of Eld Ashara)
- Few mountain passes exist through the range

**Connected Locations:** [[Ashen Vale]], [[Ashbrook]], [[Ashen Flow]]


### The Bonewall

**Type:** Region (Mountain Range)

The eastern mountain range forming part of the L-shaped border.
Dry and cold climate.
Barren, rocky, windswept, with sparse vegetation.
Feeds the [[Eastern Rivers]] through deep springs and aquifers.
Less snow than the [[Cinderwall]] despite being cold.

**Connected Locations:** [[Eastern Rivers]], [[Cinderwall]]


## Cities & Kingdoms


### Beaconhold

**Type:** City / Kingdom

A modern city-state kingdom, one of many that emerged after the fall of the [[Middle Kingdoms]].
The kingdom is built around the last known intact [[Farus Lucis]] — a dormant beacon tower that serves as a symbol of ancient connection and inherited legitimacy.
The [[The Light|Luciferian faith]] is the state religion here — supported but not mandated.
Other religions are tolerated but not supported, and perhaps frowned upon.

Like other modern kingdoms, Beaconhold essentially controls its capital city and immediate surroundings.
Some kingdoms have a main city and a few minor cities.
Outside the cities and main roads lies the wild.
The kingdom may claim mountains, valleys, and regions, but only truly controls the capital, main roads, inns along those roads, and checkpoints and fortresses.

**Notable Features:**
- Last known intact [[Farus Lucis]] (dormant beacon tower)
- Seat of the crown, ruled by the King of Beaconhold (name TBD)
- The [[Ashen Vale]] and [[Eastern Rivers]] are border or hinterland regions of this kingdom

**Connected Locations:** [[Marrow County]], [[Ashen Vale]], [[Eastern Rivers]], [[Great Lake]]


### Aurelion

**Type:** City

The capital city of [[Marrow County]].
Seat of [[Count Albrecht Marrow]]'s power and center of the county's administration.

**Notable Features:**
- Home to a church led by [[Luminary Severin Morrow]], whose severe preaching has turned part of his congregation against bards and storytellers

**Connected Locations:** [[Marrow County]]


## Towns & Settlements


### Crownvale

**Type:** Town

The largest town in the [[Ashen Vale]] and seat of [[Aldric Garlandsson]]'s ambitions.
Built around the ruins of an old [[Imperium Lucis Aeternae|Imperium]] fort that Aldric has been restoring as his keep, with ramshackle buildings growing up around the walls.
A farming community and market town with a bridge approach.
Aldric styles himself a lord here, basing his authority on the [[Greenholt Bloodline|Greenholt name]].
[[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]] is widely recognized here but prefers to avoid attention since Aldric's power relies on Garland's absence.

**Notable Features:**
- Political center of the [[Ashen Vale]]
- Aldric's keep built on [[Imperium Lucis Aeternae|Imperium]] ruins
- Lies directly in the path of the curse if the beaver dam on the [[Ashen Flow]] breaks

**Connected Locations:** [[Ashen Vale]], [[Ashen Flow]], [[Beaver Lake]]

**Key Events:**
- [[Session 1]] — The party stopped to resupply; Garland purchased horses while disguised; Edric distracted guards and gathered intelligence; Mira recognized Garland; Roderic delivered a blessing urging compassion


## Ruins


### Ashbrook (Eld Ashara)

**Type:** Ruins

Once a town high in the [[Cinderwall]], near one of the few mountain passes.
Originally a thriving settlement where [[Garland yn Greenholt]]'s relatives lived.
Now a cursed ruin known as Eld Ashara, aged centuries in moments by the spreading curse.

The spring beneath the town feeds the Upper [[Ashen Flow]], carrying the curse downstream toward the [[Ashen Vale]].
Before the fall, residents spoke of the town "building itself" and there was talk of renaming it to something grander.

**Notable Features:**
- Location of the reemergence of the God of Ruin
- The [[Seal of Unmaking]] was recovered from here by the party, unwittingly releasing what it contained
- A spring beneath the ruins now carries the curse into the [[Ashen Flow]]

**Connected Locations:** [[Cinderwall]], [[Ashen Flow]], [[Ashen Vale]]


### Altreth

**Type:** Ruins

A city-state that once clung to the side of Mount Ripea.
It was a prosperous metropolis whose wealth came from rich veins of ore carved from the mountain's depths.

When a trading caravan arrived after a particularly long winter, they found the city already dead.
Altreth had not fallen to fire or flood, but to a deeper failure: the bedrock beneath it had decayed, and the mountain itself slumped over the city like a lifeless body.

Nearly all of Altreth was buried beneath stone and rubble.
Only one structure remained untouched: the great church in the central square.
Upon its altar rested a single [[Whiteglass]] disc — the [[Seal of Unmaking]].
Those who recovered the relic reportedly aged with unnatural speed and soon died.
Their homes fell to ruin, and even their names were forgotten.
The relic vanished after the initial recovery and eventually surfaced in [[Ashbrook]].


## Landmarks & Waterways


### Beaver Lake

**Type:** Landmark

A lake in the foothills of the [[Cinderwall]], formed by the Great Beaver Dam.
The upper [[Ashen Flow]] feeds into it, and the lower [[Ashen Flow]] continues beyond the dam into the [[Ashen Vale]].
Home to a beaver colony that [[Castor]] knows well — his kin built and maintained the dam for generations.
The beavers were evacuated downstream when the party arrived ahead of the ruin.

**Notable Features:**
- The Great Beaver Dam — a massive beaver-built dam that has stood for over 120 years
- An abandoned fisher's hut on the lakeshore, where the party rested overnight
- [[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]]'s spirit wall erected upstream of the lake to filter the ruin from the water
- The corruption is pooling at the barrier site, creating a growing concentration of ruinous energy

**Connected Locations:** [[Cinderwall]], [[Ashen Flow]], [[Ashen Vale]]

**Key Events:**
- [[Session 1]] — Castor evacuated the beaver colony; Garland erected a spirit wall; Roderic's healing failed and horses withered; Garland found a scrap of Aldric's livery in the reeds


### The Ashen Flow

**Type:** Wilderness (River)

A river that flows from the [[Cinderwall]] mountains through the [[Ashen Vale]].
The giant lake that feeds the river is miles away, well beyond the borders of the Ashen Vale.
While the water runs clear from the lake, it grows murky and unpleasant as it comes downstream.
Yet by the time it reaches the Ashen Vale, the water is clean — as pure as fresh rainwater.

**Upper Ashen Flow:** The upper portion originates from a spring beneath [[Ashbrook]].
It is fed by several other rivers and springs from the [[Cinderwall]].
Since the curse, this section carries the aging curse downstream.

**The Great Beaver Dam:** Along the river stands a beaver dam that has existed as long as anyone remembers, never grown rotten.
The dam blocks the Upper Ashen Flow, forming a lake, and filters the water below.
The dam currently holds back the cursed water from [[Ashbrook]], but the dam and the beavers maintaining it are aging rapidly under the curse's influence.

**Lower Ashen Flow:** The clean, calm portion of the river that runs through the [[Ashen Vale]], filtered by the beaver dam upstream.

**Connected Locations:** [[Ashbrook]], [[Cinderwall]], [[Ashen Vale]], [[Crownvale]]


### Farlands Pass

**Type:** Landmark

A mountain pass through the [[Cinderwall]] connecting the [[Ashen Vale]] region to lands beyond.
Used by trade caravans.

**Connected Locations:** [[Cinderwall]], [[Ashen Vale]]


### The Great Lake

**Type:** Region (Body of Water)

A large freshwater lake fed by the [[Eastern Rivers]].
Multiple city-states and kingdoms line its shores.
[[Beaconhold]]'s port city provides access to lake trade.
Accessible through the [[Cragmarr]] via the river passages.

**Connected Locations:** [[Eastern Rivers]], [[Cragmarr]], [[Beaconhold]]
