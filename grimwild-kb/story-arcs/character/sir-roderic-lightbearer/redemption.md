# Redemption

**Type:** Character ([[Sir Roderic Lightbearer]])
**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active
**Started:** [[Session 1]]

## Summary
[[Sir Roderic Lightbearer|Roderic]]'s crisis of faith deepened during the race to save the [[Ashen Vale]].
His attempt to heal the exhausted horses at [[Beaver Lake]] was overwhelmed by ruin's proximity — the horses withered and aged before his eyes.
He interpreted this as confirmation that his faith is insufficient, the same weakness he blames for his battalion's destruction.

He is on assignment from the [[The Light]] as a knight errant of "The Redeemers of Light," specifically tasked with redemption through deeds.
His successful blessings in the [[Crownvale]] tavern and his commanding invocation of Luciferian authority against the Aureate offer a counterpoint: his faith can still move people and compel mortal forces, even if it cannot yet overcome the [[Old Gods|old gods]]' power.

Ruin struck at Roderic's core identity as a protector — his door lock crumbled in his hands, and ley line energy passing through him emerged corrupted.
He learned the Light's ancient protections along the [[Bonewall]] are crumbling.
He has begun theorizing about a larger conspiracy involving [[Luminary Severin Morrow|Severin]] and [[Count Albrecht Marrow|Marrow]], deepening his understanding of the threat.

## Open Questions
- Can Roderic's faith overcome the influence of ruin, or is Lucifer's power insufficient against the old gods?

- Will the shrine of Renewal beneath the Chryseum challenge or strengthen his faith?
- Is there a conspiracy between [[Luminary Severin Morrow|Severin]] and [[Count Albrecht Marrow|Marrow]]?
