# Marrow County

**Type:** Region
**First Visited:** [[Session 0]]

## Description
A minor but strategically placed county between [[Beaconhold]] county and the [[Ashen Vale]].
Ruled by [[Count Albrecht Marrow]].
Deeply entwined with the [[The Light]].
Its capital is [[Aurelion]].
Once an independent kingdom called Aurelion, it was absorbed by [[Beaconhold|Beacon Hold]] during the kingdom's expansion after the fracturing of the [[Middle Kingdoms]].

## Notable Features
- Strategically positioned between the capital and the Ashen Vale
- The Count is a major donor to the Church, with several sons in influential clerical positions
- The Aureate (Count Marrow's soldiers) patrol the roads and enforce official orders, including a crackdown against bards and storytellers ordered by [[Luminary Severin Morrow]]
- [[The Nodrum]] is located deep in the county, not far from [[Aurelion]]
