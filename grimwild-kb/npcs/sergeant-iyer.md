# Sergeant Iyer

**First Appeared:** [[Session 1]]
**Status:** Active
**Affiliation:** [[Aldric Garlandsson]], [[Crownvale]]

## Description
A sergeant-at-arms in [[Aldric Garlandsson|Aldric]]'s guard, responsible for escorting [[Mira]].
A dutiful soldier with personal troubles — his wife has been unfaithful.

## Role
[[Edric Bloom|Edric]] charmed him into abandoning his post briefly for drinks and pumped him for information about [[Aldric Garlandsson|Aldric]].
The information he provided suggests Aldric was not directly involved in the ruin's release.

## Relationships
- [[Aldric Garlandsson]] — His lord and commander
- [[Mira]] — In his charge as escort
- [[Edric Bloom]] — Charmed into sharing information over drinks

## Key Events
- [[Session 1]] — Edric bought him drinks and extracted information about Aldric; Edric experienced a disturbing compulsion to weaponize knowledge of Iyer's personal troubles
