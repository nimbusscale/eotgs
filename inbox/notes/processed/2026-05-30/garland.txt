When garland originally re-settled the vale, he had a huge and prolific family, which went on to repopulate the area, along with people who immigrated from nearby areas. His wife aged and died like a normal human, and even his longest-lived original offspring are (probably) gone by the onset of the story.

About a hundred years ago, something drew him away from the vale. During that journey, which took him far from the vale, he was responsible for the death of a man he had become friends with while dealing with whatever drew him to that place. When he eventually returned to the vale, he brought his friend's widow and child along with him. They eventually married, resulting in a second family, and that's where Aldric (and the granddaughter who died at eld ashara) came from.
