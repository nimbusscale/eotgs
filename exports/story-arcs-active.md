# Active Story Arcs


## Group Arcs


### Curse of Ruin

**Type:** Group
**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active
**Started:** Session 0

#### Summary
The party was hired by Count Albrecht Marrow to retrieve a twisted holy symbol from ruins in the mountains above the Ashen Vale.
What should have been a straightforward job turned into something far worse.
The ruins — Ashbrook, now called Eld Ashara — were not ancient; they had been a thriving settlement where Garland yn Greenholt's great-grandchild Rowan yn Greenholt lived, now aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse.

The symbol itself was the Seal of Unmaking, and by removing it, the party unwittingly released the God of Ruin it contained.
Now the curse spreads through the mountain springs, flowing downward toward the valley.
Garland erected a spirit wall at Beaver Lake to hold back the corruption, but the ruin is pooling at the barrier site and will eventually overwhelm it.
The beaver colony was evacuated downstream by Castor.

The party realizes they were used — whoever hired Edric Bloom knew exactly what would happen.
They are no longer just adventurers; they are the only ones who can make things right.
The party has decided not to return the Seal of Unmaking to Marrow and is heading to Beacon Hold to seek help from the Church of Lucifer and the Wizard's College.

Ruin's influence is spreading beyond the river — rotting preserved food, crumbling structures, and subtly affecting people's minds, making them accept decay and endings as natural.

#### Open Questions
- Who or what is the God of Ruin?
- Why did Count Albrecht Marrow want the seal broken?
- How can the curse be stopped?
- Can the spirit wall hold long enough for the party to find a solution at Beacon Hold?
- What will happen to Crownvale if the barrier fails?
- What is the connection between Aldric Garlandsson's livery and the stranger who visited Ashbrook before the curse?
- Is ruin's influence specifically targeting the party? (Edric's compulsion in the tavern)
- Can the Seal of Unmaking be used to reseal the force of ruin?
- What happened to the Imperium that even with the Unmaker sealed and Lucifer ascendant, the empire still fell?
- Does Marrow's son in the church hierarchy mean the church in Marrow County cannot be trusted?

#### Key Events
- Session 0 — Party retrieved the Seal of Unmaking from Ashbrook, releasing the God of Ruin; curse now spreading via the Ashen Flow
- Session 1 — Party raced ahead of the cursed river; evacuated the beaver colony; erected a spirit wall at Beaver Lake (temporary); encountered ruin's influence on ordinary people and on Edric's mind; departed Crownvale for Beacon Hold

#### Related Entities
- Seal of Unmaking — The artifact that imprisoned the God of Ruin
- Ashbrook — The town destroyed by the curse (now Eld Ashara)
- Ashen Flow — The river carrying cursed water downstream
- Beaver Lake — Location of Garland's spirit wall barrier
- Count Albrecht Marrow — Commissioned the retrieval; his motives are suspect
- Crownvale — At risk if the spirit wall fails
- Rowan yn Greenholt — Garland's great-grandchild who perished in Ashbrook
- Dire Coyotes — Shapeshifters of the Wild Hunt who confronted the party in the Cinderwall


## Character Arcs


### Garland: Uncover the Truth

**Type:** Character (Garland yn Greenholt)
**Theme:** Discover the Truth
**Status:** Active
**Started:** Session 1

#### Summary
Garland chose "Uncover the Truth" as his character arc, driven by suspicion that his grandson Aldric may be connected to the events at Ashbrook.
Evidence is mounting: Rowan's letter mentioned someone in Aldric's livery near Ashbrook before the disaster, and a fresh scrap of Aldric's livery was found at Beaver Lake.
However, Edric's investigation of Aldric's guards suggests Aldric himself is uninvolved.
The mystery remains — who bearing his colors was present at these locations?

#### Open Questions
- Who was wearing Aldric's livery at Beaver Lake and near Ashbrook before the disaster?
- Is Aldric connected to the events at Ashbrook, or is someone using his name?
- What is the full story behind the Seal of Unmaking's retrieval?

#### Key Events
- Session 1 — Garland found Aldric's livery at Beaver Lake; Edric's investigation of Sergeant Iyer suggests Aldric is not directly involved; Garland avoided recognition in Crownvale but was spotted by Mira

#### Related Entities
- Aldric Garlandsson — Garland's grandson, under suspicion
- Rowan yn Greenholt — Garland's granddaughter who perished at Ashbrook; her letter is key evidence
- Mira — Aldric's daughter who recognized Garland
- Sergeant Iyer — Aldric's guard; Edric extracted information from him
- Beaver Lake — Where the livery scrap was found


### Roderic: Redemption

**Type:** Character (Sir Roderic Lightbearer)
**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active
**Started:** Session 1

#### Summary
Roderic's crisis of faith deepened during the race to save the Ashen Vale.
His attempt to heal the exhausted horses at Beaver Lake was overwhelmed by ruin's proximity — the horses withered and aged before his eyes.
He interpreted this as confirmation that his faith is insufficient, the same weakness he blames for his battalion's destruction.

He is on assignment from the Church of Lucifer as a knight errant of "The Redeemers of Light," specifically tasked with redemption through deeds.
His successful blessings in the Crownvale tavern — urging compassion for the confused horse seller Laura — offered a counterpoint: his faith can still move people, even if it cannot yet overcome the old gods' power.

#### Open Questions
- Can Roderic's faith overcome the influence of ruin, or is Lucifer's power insufficient against the old gods?
- What happened to Roderic's battalion, and was his faith truly to blame?
- How will seeking counsel at Beacon Hold affect his crisis of faith?

#### Key Events
- Session 1 — Failed to heal horses (ruin overwhelmed his power); successfully delivered a blessing in Crownvale defending the horse seller Laura; warned Edric that endorsing "new beginnings through destruction" feeds ruin's power

#### Related Entities
- Church of Lucifer — His faith and order
- Beaconhold — Destination where he seeks counsel
- Beaver Lake — Where his healing failed
- Crownvale — Where his blessing succeeded
