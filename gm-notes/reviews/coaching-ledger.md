# GM Coaching Ledger

Running, cross-session synthesis of the `/review-session` feedback — the through-line
that no single session review can show. Read at the **start** of every review (for
continuity) and updated at the **end** (with that session's deltas).

Scope is **Chasing Adventure / PbtA craft only** — running *this* system better,
session over session. Not general GMing, not table logistics.

---

## Recurring strengths

_Patterns of strong CA play that show up across multiple sessions. Cite the sessions._

- **Fictional positioning & intent** — asks "what's your goal?"/"what do you want to
  happen?" before locking a Move and lets the fiction pick the Defy stat instead of
  defaulting (S3: Garland enduring +STR; S4: the sword-heave correction at 1099-1135,
  Garland heaving +STR vs. +DEX; table adopted "describe the outcome, not the action").
- **Portray a Lively World** — NPCs and even animals pursue their own goals; ruin shown
  in down-to-earth detail (S3: faded Aurelion, Dorn's mended cloak, Tufa's rat society;
  S4: Brenn/Severin's own theology and agency, the Harlequin who picks his own lock and
  bolts, moss in the floor-cracks and torches shifting to white light).
- **Spotlight distribution** — _graduated from growth area in S4._ Explicit, frequent
  spotlight passes across a multi-PC scene and active pull-ins for the quiet player
  (S4: "moving the spotlight to Garland," checking in with Castor at 1027/2260; GM mic
  time 58.5% → 53.7%).
- **Collaborative "Ask Them" worldbuilding** — turning exposition into player-owned
  fiction via "how would you know?" and direct asks (S3: herald-impact prompt, signature-
  weapon ask, the Dorn-flashback vantage). _Note: under-used in S4's long debate, where
  exposition replaced asks._
- **Leave Things Open / play to find out** — runs unprepared content and folds player
  improvisation into canon (S3: the whole mine route, Tufa improvised into a lasting arc;
  S4: herald identity left genuinely open, Garland's player-driven spellbook beat canonized).

## Recurring growth areas

_System-specific habits to work on that recur across sessions. Cite the sessions,
and note whether each is trending better, worse, or flat._

- **Think Dangerously — no Hard Move on 6-/golden opportunities** (S3, S4) — _trending
  flat._ Two reviewed sessions, zero Hard Moves. S4: clean 6- on Roderic's Engage softened
  (pushed back, reversible, no condition); a declined 7-9 ugly choice let settle into a
  stalemate instead of escalating; no condition inflicted on any PC; the inquisitor/mob/
  alarm threats around the prison break all went uncashed (table itself called it "no
  repercussions"). **Top priority.**
- **Pacing / Gloss Over — long stretches past their tension** (S4) — _new._ Prison-key
  sequence and a ~35-min renewal/ruin debate both ran as near-pure exposition with no
  Move, roll, or stated clock spent (GM self-diagnosed). Tie to Think Dangerously: a scene
  with a live timer (mob at the doors) wants a Make Them Choose, not background noise.
- **State the threat before the roll** (S3) — _trending up, nearly resolved._ S4 front-
  loaded the threat on Castor's key-grab and self-corrected on Garland's sneak.
- **Consequence variety beyond conditions** (S3, S4) — _flat-to-slightly-up._ S4 had one
  good non-condition consequence (shattered sword = Take Something Away) but otherwise soft;
  reach for alarms raised, sealed exits, environment shifts, Forces advancing.
- **Re-anchoring ill-fitting drives** (S3, S4) — Castor's drive still doesn't fit and he'll
  "think on it" (S4 close); drives are at least being credited at End of Session now.
- ~~Spotlight distribution in long solo scenes~~ (S3) — **resolved in S4**, moved to
  Recurring strengths.

## Open recommendations (carry forward)

_Concrete, in-system suggestions made in past reviews that haven't yet been
demonstrably adopted. Each review checks these against the new transcript and marks
them ✅ adopted / ◐ partial / ⬚ not yet, with evidence._

- **[S3, still open] On the next clean 6- or golden opportunity, make a *Hard* Move** —
  something that changes the fiction and can't be walked back (raise the alarm, seal an
  exit, drag a PC off, inflict a condition), played out fully before "what do you do?"
  _Not adopted in S4 — the single most important carry-forward._
- **[S4] When a player declines the 7-9 ugly choice, escalate — don't let it settle into a
  stalemate.** The threat does not abate; inflict a condition and advance it.
- **[S4] Spend the scene's stated clock.** When a timer is named (mob at the doors, an
  inquisitor closing in), convert it into a *Make Them Choose* or *Set Up an Immediate
  Danger* instead of leaving it as ambience — especially to cut a long exposition scene.
- **[S4] Turn the renewal/ruin-style debate into a Paladin Virtue test for Roderic** —
  name when the fiction collides with a vow (Purity/Honor) so the talk becomes his crisis.
- **[S3, partial] Re-anchor ill-fitting drives** — Castor's still doesn't fit; frame an
  early beat that pulls him toward a sharper, self-chosen drive and name it when he hits it.
- ~~[S3] Name the danger before every Defy~~ — **largely adopted in S4.**
- ~~[S3] Cut away to give the quiet player a decision~~ — **adopted in S4.**
- ~~[S3] Hook Garland's *Wizard* identity~~ — **landed in S4** (cocoon spellbook beat).
