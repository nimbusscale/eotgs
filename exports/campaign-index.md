# Echoes of the Godstorm - Campaign Index

## Campaign Overview

The party was hired by Count Albrecht Marrow to retrieve a twisted holy symbol from ruins in the mountains above the Ashen Vale.
The ruins were not ancient; they were Ashbrook, a thriving settlement where Garland's great-grandchild Rowan had lived, aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse.
The symbol itself was the Seal of Unmaking, and by removing it the party unwittingly released the God of Ruin it contained.
The Triune revealed that the party were the heralds of ruin — carrying the curse in their bodies rather than in the water — and that ruin had a counterpart in a long-dormant God of Renewal whose absence had thrown the natural cycle out of balance.

The Morrow family revealed themselves as secret worshippers of renewal: Luminary Severin, Count Marrow, and Dawnwarden Brenn are the heralds of renewal, counterparts to the party's heralds of ruin.
The party retrieved Count Marrow from besieged Crest Aurelion during the Order of the Eclipsed Sword's siege, and all six heralds gathered at the Shrine of Renewal beneath the Chryseum.
The cocoon opened, releasing a spring-like surge that transformed the desiccated chamber into a verdant, living space; the Dark Harlequin was beheaded by Roderic after attempting to prevent the reunion, and Edric vanished mid-step beneath an invisibility spell in the tunnels below, his whereabouts unknown.
The balance between ruin and renewal was restored within the Shrine, but the broader world still bore the scars: First Aureate Eisen Dorn fell defending Crest Aurelion, Aurelion struck a grudging truce with the Morrows, the Great Beaver Dam partially collapsed and its beavers spread downstream as pests, Aldric Garlandsson began consolidating power across the vale, and cults of whispered endings took root in Crownvale and southern Marrow County.

Now something is being forgotten at a scale larger than the curse any one of them once carried.
Along the spine of the Bonewall the wards that anchor the Thirteen Spines are failing — the rites that once renewed them lost when Sariel's erasure unmade part of the Light's history, so the wards decay untended and unremembered — and Carnforth, the northernmost Spine, has gone fully dark as something from Öuth Krelt presses against what remains.
The faithful of the Light are vanishing from their beds in Beaconhold, among them Roderic's mentor Primus Griswald, while the hero Sariel is being unmade from the Church's records and its clergy's memory — though Roderic alone still remembers him.
At a failing Spine called Hallowreach, a Spinewarden named Paxton Lumnus died anchoring the Light in a sacrificial ritual, then woke in the mausoleum with the Light burning in his heart and a vision that the wards themselves are flawed.

The party's three scattered roads have met underground, in Muistola — "the Remembrance," the hidden town of the Slate — a people of living stone whose ageless, deathless, birthless stasis has just broken with the first child born among them in living memory.
There the Slate's memory stones gave up truths older than the Imperium: the wall's wards were built not by Lucifer but by Vaalo, a deep dwarf of Syvämaa who drew their fire from the light of the world below and carried it in the lantern Hiillos — Vaalo himself erased from scripture as wholly as Sariel was, and the thirteen spines rekindlable only by retrieving Hiillos from his cradle in the Tomb of the Spinewright.
And then Moldrex tore the town in two, offering to sever the Slate from Syvämaa's cycle of emergence and petrification in exchange for the newborn child Itu — splitting the people between those who would carry the child to the dark god and those who would not, as his vines and spiders broke into Muistola and the party fought to keep the child.

## Player Characters

### Castor
**Player:** Dustin (duskit)
**Concept:** A druid who spent an extraordinarily long time cursed as a beaver, now more beaver-in-human-skin than human. He carries the Greenholt Bloodline and serves as the party's scout, utility caster, and shapeshifter.
**Key Abilities:** Shapeshift (Old Wild background — any encountered creature, not only Attuned; beaver form always free), Attunement, Commune, At One With The World, Bridge Between Worlds (Advanced — extends his bond to plants, rocks, and the raw elements, the source of his power to dissolve into wind and move "between worlds"); beaver is his true form
**Relationships:** Regards Garland as a father figure despite being technically older (Garland is in truth his great-grand-nephew); lowkey camaraderie with Edric; growing respect for Roderic; has taken a liking to young Mira, who calls him "Pop pop"
**Current Threads:** The origin and motive of his original beaver curse remain unknown — the Dark Harlequin claimed the Laughing One was responsible, but the source is unreliable; his family never seemed to remember him after he was cursed, and why is unresolved; the Beaver Lake colony has fractured under the upstart Tamarack and spread downstream as pests, souring the vale's great families against it and bringing armed men to demand Castor be handed over; he broke a centuries-held vow of celibacy with Tufa the rock rat, and whether his druidic curse can pass to offspring is open; a renewed curiosity and wanderlust have stirred a late-life desire to learn what his mitigated curse is truly capable of — passing through Mira's portal in wind form gave him a fleeting vision of his own lost life as a young carpenter before the centuries of beaver, and the Slate's memory stones sharpened that grief into a push from spectator toward someone who acts, answered when he leapt in as a gorilla to save the child Itu; and the God of Renewal's vision named him a protector and a crucial piece of the balance — what that role demands is unresolved

### Garland yn Greenholt
**Player:** Jay (regular human faits)
**Concept:** An extraordinarily old warrior-sage entering his "third act," who carries the Greenholt Bloodline that grants elven features and an extended lifespan. He is a former soldier, accidental lord, and guardian of the Witch Stones. He once knew deep magic, but the Curse of Ruin — his burden as the herald of forgetting — stripped most of it away, leaving a frontline fighter slowly recovering his arcane knowledge.
**Key Abilities:** Signature Weapon (Second Harvest), Battle Momentum, Particular Set of Skills, Armed and Ready, cross-class Wizard (Evoke a Spell, Prestidigitation, Ritual), Known Spells: Immunity and Invisibility; Veteran background (battlefield intuition)
**Relationships:** Complex guardianship of Castor; growing camaraderie with Roderic; lowkey affection for Edric; grandson Aldric rules Crownvale; great-grandchild Rowan perished in Ashbrook; great-great-grandchild Mira calls him "Pappy"/"Dagar" and has declared herself his apprentice; now owes a bond of obligation to Sergeant Iyer, whom he Favored to follow Mira through the portal; newly reunited with Paxton, whose resurrection-sigil he has privately recognized as the mark of the "unborn"
**Current Threads:** A renewed bond with the Witch Stones is returning his magic without restoring lost knowledge, and Mira's gate-craft opened a stable portal onto a matching ring on the Bonewall — a second ring twin to his own but carved in an unreadable runic language and worn nearly silent; comparing notes with the Slate sage Kallio confirmed the stones form a ley-line network spanning the Ashen Vale, the Cragmar, the Bonewall and beyond, and after passing through the realm of the forgotten he now hears the stones clearly, in words, as Mira does; he means to reproduce Mira's long-distance gate as a ritual to send her safely home; he wants to understand Castor's curse well enough to undo it before his "third act" ends; a Starfall Caravan Company officer named Cassian Vellor suspects him of being a fraud and is investigating; the buried chapter of the hundred-year journey from which Aldric's line springs remains unspoken; the Greenholt Bloodline's origin is unknown, though the memory stones showed his own line raising the Witch Stones, giving a child over to them, and a descendant opening an ancient vale civilization's gates to invaders; to pull Mira free of the realm of the forgotten he sacrificed the memory of his first son, Dafydd, and now carries only a grief he cannot name; and Aldric is consolidating power across the vale with an unnatural reach — somehow knowing all that happens within it, watched by ravens that are not quite ravens — building toward a reckoning

### Sir Roderic Lightbearer
**Player:** Ramsey (feklars)
**Concept:** A Luciferian paladin and war orphan turned knight-errant, devoted to protecting the weak and bringing light. He serves as the party's moral anchor and frontline defender.
**Key Abilities:** Devout Virtues (Courage, Honor, Mercy, Purity, Justice), Decree (divine command), Chains of Faith, Armed and Ready, Healing Hands (Guardian background — two effects), Eyes of the Faithful (custom), Divine Favor (Advanced — Cleric Miracles, including Illuminate)
**Relationships:** Playful camaraderie with Edric; deep respect for Garland; lowkey doubts about Castor; newly met Paxton in the Slate's town; his vanished mentor Primus Griswald recruited and spared him; an uneasy alliance with Corona Vigil inquisitor Voss, who once branded him a heretic and now charges him to investigate the failing wards; a longstanding boyhood contact in Vicar Lucis Gregory
**Current Threads:** His company of paladins died in the Tomb of the Spinewright seeking the Spinewright's Lantern — now revealed as Hiillos, Vaalo's lantern of the light below, whose retrieval could rekindle the failing spines — and he woke amid their mutilated bodies remembering nothing, was cast out as the suspected sole survivor, and the one man who knew his innocence, Griswald, has now vanished; the tomb is buried under a fresh rockslide and a figure named Roland sends lantern-marked men to keep him from it; he was branded a heretic during Count Marrow's extraction, though the Vigil withdrew and no charges followed him home; his nuanced theology — ruin and renewal as forces of nature subordinate to Lucifer, not gods — stands in tension with the Order of the Eclipsed Sword's orthodoxy; where Beaconhold's clergy can no longer recall the hero Sariel, Roderic remembers him clearly — and at the memory stones he sensed a second figure, as important as Lucifer and distinct from Sariel, likewise erased from all he was ever taught, perhaps the deep dwarf Vaalo or another; why the forgetting has not touched him is an open question

### Paxton Lumnus
**Player:** Ken (SiliKen)
**Concept:** A resurrected Spinewarden who died anchoring the Light at a failing ward and woke carrying that Light in his heart. He is an embodiment of radiance who no longer trusts the institutions that wield it, driven to uncover the truth of the Light before more people die trusting a flawed protection.
**Key Abilities:** Immolator reskinned for the Light — Dawn Unending (Heart of the Phoenix; returns from death when the party next Settles In, changed a little each time), Lightbrand (shapes and creates fiery light, with the Restrained and Spontaneous traits), Unleash Radiance (fights with light as a weapon at a cost), Drawn to the Light (gains Charm through intimate moments)
**Relationships:** Newly reunited with the party in the Slate's town — he served alongside the Spinewarden Senna, whom he killed when he burned her barge in the Reaches after learning she had been reborn in the vision of Moldrex; met Roderic for the first time; rejoined Garland (who has privately recognized his resurrection-sigil) and Castor; now owes a Slate guard of Muistola a promise to keep the boulder-warrior Lohka alive
**Current Threads:** The dawn showed him that the Bonewall wards are flawed — that Lucifer did not protect Beaconhold indefinitely but only prolonged its inevitable ruin — and the memory stones gave him the first real answer: the wards were Vaalo's work, not Lucifer's, drawing their fire from the light below through the lantern Hiillos, which must be retrieved from Vaalo's cradle in the Tomb of the Spinewright to rekindle the dark spines; he means to learn whether the Light's legacy can be repaired or must be exposed; he has "died" several times since the ritual and it never takes, each return leaving him subtly different; he is too quick to treat his own survival, pain, and death as expendable, a recklessness that burned Roy and unseen captives aboard Senna's barge — wading back he could save only the woman and child and left her husband to the flames — and that others keep paying for; and he owes a debt of favor to a party member his Light has already burned

## Active Story Arcs

### Group: The Forgotten and the Forsaken
**Theme:** TBD
**Status:** Active

The cocoon opened and the world breathed, but balance is not peace.
Renewal has awakened within the Shrine, yet ruin's passage has left scars no ceremony can heal — and now something is being forgotten at a scale larger than the curse any one of them once carried.
Along the Bonewall the wards are failing, the faithful are vanishing, and the hero Sariel is being unmade from memory and record.
The party were scattered and converging on the Bonewall, each finding the same rot wearing a different face.

In Session 6, three roads led toward the wall.
Roderic returned to a Beaconhold whose Light had fractured and whose scripture had subtly changed, was lured to the imperial tunnels under Griswald's name, met Inquisitor Voss instead, and accepted the charge to investigate the failing wards while learning that the faithful — Griswald among them — had vanished and Sariel's bust had disappeared without a trace of dust.
Mira commanded the Witch Stones to tear open a portal onto a matching ring on the Bonewall's dead grey shale, and Garland and Castor followed her through.
Paxton found Senna in the Reaches healing marsh-creatures with a scaled hand and reptilian eyes, learned she had been reborn in the vision of Moldrex, and burned her barge to escape — killing Senna and a family hidden aboard.

In Session 7 the three threads finally met, deep inside the wall, in Muistola, the hidden town of the Slate — a people of living stone whose ageless, deathless, birthless stasis has just broken with the first child born among them in living memory.
Paxton waded back to the burning barge and saved a woman and child but left her husband Roy to the fire, then climbed toward dark Carnforth through vines that had begun to move, resist fire, and grow monstrous, and was carried near death into the mountain by a young Slate.
Garland and Castor spilled from the portal onto the Bonewall's second ring of Witch Stones — twin to Garland's own but carved in an unreadable tongue and worn nearly silent — where Castor felt the mountain celebrating and Mira gained the grinding stone-tongue at the cost of being understood in any human language.
Roderic broke an ambush by Roland's lantern-marked men sent to keep him from the Spinewright's tomb, found it buried under a rockslide, and fell through collapsing ground into the underground river, hauled out by the Slate.
All three threads reunited in the Slate's town, where it came clear that the wards are failing as a side effect of Sariel's erasure from history — the rites that once renewed them lost along with the angel — and that the party's hand in it is indirect and already behind them: their ruin at the Nodrum let the erasure happen, but that ruin passed from them when renewal was reborn, and they are heralds of ruin no longer.

In Session 8 the memory stones of Muistola gave the party the Slate tongue and visions shaped by their own longing, unburying truths older than the Imperium — and then the town tore itself in two over a single newborn child.
The elder Holvi led them to the Hall of Deep Memory, where Paxton saw Vaalo the deep dwarf, Roderic sensed a second erased figure as vital as Lucifer, Garland watched his own bloodline raise the Witch Stones and a descendant betray an ancient vale civilization, and Castor felt a mirror of his own long stasis in the Slate's frozen ages.
Holvi told the story of Vaalo — a worshipper of the light below who climbed from Syvämaa, befriended Lucifer, and built the spines as the "children of Hiillos," his lantern carrying a shard of that light — revealing that the failing towers can be rekindled only by retrieving Hiillos from Vaalo's cradle, the very Tomb of the Spinewright Roderic came from.
Apart from the others, Garland and Mira found the stone-sage Kallio, confirmed the Witch Stones form a ley-line network spanning realms, and slipped into the blue spirit realm to mend a dimming node — the realm of the forgotten, where Garland gave up the memory of his dead son Dafydd to pull Mira free, surfacing hearing the stones clearly and grieving a loss he can no longer name.
Then the schism broke: Lohka, Kallio, and others had embraced Moldrex, who promised to sever the Slate from Syvämaa's cycle in exchange for the child Itu — and as Castor seized the child as a gorilla, Lohka snapped Roderic's sword, Paxton seared the flesh beneath Lohka's stone, and Mira raised a sanctuary on the stairs, the session ending in a three-way standstill as spiders and tentacles broke into Muistola.

**Open Questions:**
- What is the God of Renewal now that it has awakened, and how will it affect the broader world?
- Even with the Unmaker sealed and Lucifer ascendant, the Imperium still fell — what brought down the empire despite its advantages?
- The God of Forgetting created the Xan-Kor and erased Sariel from existence; what is its current status among the old gods, and its role in the present crisis?
- Sariel was erased — removed backward through time rather than killed — and his unmaking is now manifesting physically; can he be found or restored, and why does Roderic alone still remember him?
- Who is the figure as important as Lucifer, besides Sariel, that the memory stones showed Roderic has been erased from all record — is it the deep dwarf Vaalo, or another?
- The faithful of the Light are vanishing overnight from Beaconhold, Primus Griswald among them, as the Corona Vigil's dread withers to nothing — did the missing leave willingly or were they taken, and how does it connect to the unraveling of scripture?
- Something is missing from the Light's own traditions, histories, and hymns — what was lost, and can it be found?
- Can the lantern Hiillos be retrieved from Vaalo's cradle in the Tomb of the Spinewright and the failing spines reignited from it?
- Who is Roland, why is he so set on keeping Roderic from the Tomb of the Spinewright, and what now lies buried in it — and does the lantern necklace mark his men and a Carnforth chaplain as a cult, a splinter order, or wardens of Hiillos?
- Why are the Bonewall's grey vines mutating — moving, resisting fire, growing huge, and harboring giant spiders — and is it Öuth Krelt's corruption seeping through the failing wards?
- What is Syvämaa — the deep world and its nameless under-god — and why have the Slate come to feel it has abandoned them?
- Why has the Slate's long stasis — no births, deaths, or aging — broken now, an echo of the God of Renewal's awakening, and what is the destiny of the child Itu that both factions of the schism would claim?
- Will Lohka's faction carry the child Itu through the Suonet to Moldrex's emissary in Öuth Krelt?
- Can what is lost in the realm of the forgotten — Garland's son Dafydd, the suppressed Witch Stone, Mira's missing fragment — ever be recovered?
- Do the Witch Stones form a network spanning the Ashen Vale, the Cragmar, the Bonewall and beyond, and why did the ancient stone-raisers give a child over to them?
- The beavers of Beaver Lake have become a growing problem in the Ashen Vale — can the rift between the colony and the vale's people be healed?
- Aldric Garlandsson is consolidating power beyond Crownvale and somehow knows everything that happens within the vale — how far will it go, and what is the nature of his unnatural reach?
- Count Marrow harbors resentment toward the party and watches Aldric's expansion with cold attention — what does he intend?
- Cults of whispered endings have taken root in Crownvale and southern Marrow County — are they an aftershock of ruin, or something new?
- First Aureate Dorn is dead — who now keeps order in Aurelion?
- Where is Edric, and is he still alive?
