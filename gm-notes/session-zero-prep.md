# Session Zero — Prep Notes & Player Q&A

See also: [[Curse of Ruin]]

## Session Zero Q&A Transcript

### Questions to Ken (Edric's Player)

**Q:** The "Twisted Symbol" is more than a run-of-the-mill holy symbol for the God of Ruin.
What is its name and what is the legend about the last great thing that it brought to ruin?

**A:** The legend of [[Altreth]] — a city-state clinging to the side of Mount Ripea.
Its bedrock decayed, and the mountain slumped over the city.
Only the great church remained, with a [[Whiteglass]] disc on its altar — the [[Seal of Unmaking]].
Those who recovered it aged and died; their names were forgotten.

**Q:** Who hired you to retrieve the holy symbol?

**A:** [[Count Albrecht Marrow]], lord of [[Marrow County]].
A minor lord deeply entwined with the [[Church of Lucifer]], major donor, sons in clerical positions.
Uses reputation erosion through sermons rather than open punishment.

**Q:** What makes it difficult to confront this person?

**A:** His station, Church connections, and methods.
The warding-locked container (absent from previous commissions) and lack of local information made it clear in hindsight that Marrow knew what they were retrieving.

### Questions to Jay (Garland's Player)

**Q:** Who was your relative in the town, and what did they say before things went wrong?

**A:** [[Rowan yn Greenholt]] (Rowan yn Greenholt ap Hawkworth), Garland's great-grandchild.
The town was [[Ashbrook]]; the ruins are called [[Eld Ashara]].
Her letter mentioned a stranger with [[Aldric Garlandsson|Aldric]]'s livery, changing stonework, sweet water, and talk of renaming the town.

**Q:** What's at risk if the beaver dam breaks?

**A:** [[Crownvale]], seat of power of grandson [[Aldric Garlandsson]], would-be petty king of the vale.

### Questions to Dustin (Castor's Player)

**Q:** Name of the river from the spring to the beaver dam?

**A:** The [[Ashen Flow]].
The water runs murky from the lake upstream but clean by the time it reaches the [[Ashen Vale]], filtered by the beaver dam.

**Q:** What great danger lurks in these mountains?

**A:** [[Dire Coyotes]] — a tougher, more aggressive breed in the treacherous mountain areas.
Few humans know of them.
"We all remember when Phillip talked a big game about going to the mountains to prove these particularly nasty coyotes existed.
And that's the last we heard about Phillip."
