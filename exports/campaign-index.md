# Echoes of the Godstorm - Campaign Index

## Campaign Overview

The party was hired by Count Albrecht Marrow to retrieve a twisted holy symbol from ruins in the mountains above the Ashen Vale.
What should have been a straightforward job turned into something far worse.
The ruins were not ancient; they had been a thriving settlement called Ashbrook where Garland's great-grandchild Rowan lived, now aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse.
The symbol itself was the Seal of Unmaking, and by removing it, the party unwittingly released the God of Ruin it contained.

At the Nodrum, the Triune revealed that the party themselves are the heralds of ruin — carrying the curse wherever they go, not through the water.
Ruin targets each person's core identity: protection (Roderic), knowledge (Garland), inspiration (Edric), creation (Castor).
The Triune also revealed that ruin had a counterpart — the God of Renewal — whose absence makes the current catastrophe so devastating.
The Harlequins, followers of the God of Trickery, have been following the party since Ashbrook, manipulating events for the Laughing One's entertainment.

Beneath the Chryseum in Aurelion, the party discovered the Shrine of Renewal — an ancient, pre-Luciferian chamber containing the dormant God of Renewal sleeping inside a translucent cocoon.
The Morrow family revealed themselves as secret worshippers of renewal: Luminary Severin, Count Albrecht Marrow, and Dawnwarden Brenn are the heralds of renewal, counterparts to the party's heralds of ruin.
Five heralds felt an interlocking connection at the shrine, but the pattern is incomplete — they need Count Marrow to complete the balance.
When heralds of ruin and renewal travel together, a fragile equilibrium emerges — cracks seal, moss grows — but the balance cannot hold without all six.
The Order of the Eclipsed Sword, the Church of Lucifer's own military order, has arrived at Crest Aurelion to arrest the very man the party needs most.
Edric has vanished while under an invisibility spell in the tunnels beneath the Chryseum, his whereabouts unknown.

**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active

The ruins — Ashbrook, now called Eld Ashara — were not ancient; they had been a thriving settlement where Garland's great-grandchild Rowan lived, now aged centuries in moments by a spreading curse.

The party realizes they were used — the Morrow family hired Edric knowing exactly what would happen, believing the release of ruin was necessary to awaken the dormant God of Renewal.
At the Nodrum, the Triune revealed that ruin had a counterpart — the God of Renewal — whose absence makes the current catastrophe so devastating.
Centuries of pent-up ruinous energy were released all at once.
The Triune identified the party as the heralds of ruin — carrying the curse wherever they go, not through the water.

The heralds of renewal have been found: Luminary Severin Morrow, Count Albrecht Marrow, and Dawnwarden Brenn.

### Character: Garland yn Greenholt - Uncover the Truth
**Theme:** Discover the Truth
**Status:** Active

Garland chose "Uncover the Truth" as his character arc, driven by suspicion that his grandson Aldric may be connected to the events at Ashbrook.
The Aldric livery mystery has been resolved: the Harlequin Jimmy confessed that all the livery scraps were planted by the Harlequins as a trick to exploit Garland's paranoia, largely exonerating Aldric.
However, this only proves the livery was planted — not that Aldric is entirely uninvolved in broader events.
The Morrow family revealed themselves as secret worshippers of renewal who deliberately hired the party to release ruin so that renewal could return, though the Laughing One has corrupted the process.
Garland's spellbook responded to residual renewal energy at the Shrine of Renewal, partially counteracting the forgetting curse and offering the first real hope of recovering lost knowledge.

**Open Questions:**
- Is there more to Aldric's role beyond what the Harlequins have revealed?
- Can the renewal energy fully restore the knowledge stolen by the forgetting curse?

### Character: Sir Roderic Lightbearer - Redemption
**Theme:** Make Things Right
**Status:** Active

Roderic's crisis of faith deepened during the race to save the Ashen Vale.
His attempt to heal the exhausted horses at Beaver Lake was overwhelmed by ruin's proximity — the horses withered and aged before his eyes.
He interpreted this as confirmation that his faith is insufficient, the same weakness he blames for his battalion's destruction.
He is on assignment from the Church of Lucifer as a knight errant of "The Redeemers of Light," specifically tasked with redemption through deeds.
His successful blessings in the Crownvale tavern and his commanding invocation of Luciferian authority against the Aureate offer a counterpoint: his faith can still move people and compel mortal forces, even if it cannot yet overcome the old gods' power.
Ruin struck at Roderic's core identity as a protector — his door lock crumbled in his hands, and ley line energy passing through him emerged corrupted.
Beneath the Chryseum, he demonstrated his commitment to protecting life by refusing to seriously harm the Aureate guards even when outnumbered.
His divine decree successfully commanded the Harlequin prisoners, showing his faith remains potent.
He sent intelligence about the old gods and Marrow's corruption to Beaconhold.
He faced a theological challenge when Dawnwarden Brenn called Lucifer's suppression of ruin hubris, but resolved it by interpreting his journey as divinely guided.
Now he faces his most direct crisis — the Order of the Eclipsed Sword, a military order of his own faith, stands between the party and the man they need to awaken renewal.

**Open Questions:**
- Can Roderic's faith overcome the influence of ruin, or is Lucifer's power insufficient against the old gods?
- What happened to Roderic's battalion, and was his faith truly to blame?
- How will Roderic reconcile his Luciferian faith with the revelation that Lucifer's suppression of ruin may have caused centuries of stagnation?
- Can Roderic retrieve Count Marrow from the Order of the Eclipsed Sword — his own faith's military order — without betraying his vows?
