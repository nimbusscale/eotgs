# Muistola

**Type:** Settlement (underground)
**First Visited:** [[Session 7]]

> **Muistola** — "the Remembrance" — is the Slate's own name for the town, rendered through [[Mira]]'s translation; the party know it plainly as the Slate Town.

## Description
A hidden settlement deep within the [[Bonewall]], home to [[The Slate|the Slate]] — the people of living stone who dwell inside the mountain.
It is lit only by dim bioluminescent moss and reached not by road or door but through the rock itself, which the Slate part with their hands like clay.
The name fits a people whose history is kept nowhere but in their own dead: the town is memory made into a city.

Its buildings are its dead.
The walls, the roofs, the very beds are the petrified bodies of elder Slate who, dying of old age, settle where they mean to remain and fuse into the town's architecture — ancestors literally forming the halls the living inhabit.
The Slate say their history is written in their city; they keep no records but the bodies of those who came before.

## Notable Features
- Architecture built from the petrified bodies of the Slate's own dead
- Dim bioluminescent moss for light; the Slate also use moss to heal the injured
- Reached only through solid stone, which the Slate can part with their hands

## Places Within the Town
- **The Hall of the Settled** — the town proper: a great pillared hall where every column and arch is a petrified ancestor merged into the stone, lit hollows still glowing from the celebration of the first child
- **The Cradle-Hollow** — the birthing chamber, a rounded, womb-like hollow where the rock turns soft and almost organic; warm and faintly glowing, it is where new Slate emerge from the mountain, and where [[The Slate|Aamu]] keeps watch as Keeper of Emergence
- **The Hall of Deep Memory** — walls dense with glyph-carved memory-stones in the Slate's runic script, where the oldest, most weathered ancestor-figures stand; the stones here "speak" longest, and [[The Slate|Holvi]] tends the people's deepest memory among them
- **Suonet**, "the Veins" — smooth, ancient, glyph-carved conduits, plainly built and purposeful, branching off the rough caves; they are the Slate's fast road between fixed points along the wall, threading the living mountain like veins, two running prominently north toward [[Carnforth]] and south toward [[Tomb of the Spinewright|Kehto]] (the party call them the Speedways)

## Connected Locations
- [[Bonewall]] — The wall the town is hidden within
- [[Tomb of the Spinewright]] — The buried tomb nearby, through whose collapse Roderic fell into the Slate's reach; the Slate know it as Kehto, reached now by the southern speedway

## Associated NPCs
- [[The Slate]] — The people of living stone who built and inhabit the town, among them the child Itu, the guardian Routa, the elders Kallio and Holvi, the Keeper of Emergence Aamu, and the warriors Lohka and Sora

## Events Here
- [[Session 7]] — All three of the party's threads converged here: the Slate carried in the dying [[Paxton Lumnus|Paxton]] and [[Sir Roderic Lightbearer|Roderic]], reuniting them with [[Garland yn Greenholt|Garland]], [[Castor]], and [[Mira]]; through Mira's translation the Slate told of their broken stasis and the first child born among them in living memory, and the party settled in to rest among the celebrating people
- [[Session 8]] — In the Hall of Deep Memory, [[The Slate|Holvi]] imbued the party with the Slate tongue at the memory stones, where each glimpsed a vision of the deep past; then the town's schism over the child Itu erupted into open violence as [[The Slate|Lohka]]'s [[Moldrex]] faction moved to seize the child, and [[Moldrex]]'s corruption began to breach the town — cat-sized spiders boiling over the surface ring outside and mutated vines cracking down through the ceiling as the party fought to hold a sanctuary on the stairs
