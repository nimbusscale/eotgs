Garland will have an Asset in the game which is represented by the following.

# The Starfall Caravan Company
Merchant consortium / caravan network
Garland’s original investment: A single caravan after the winter famine that starved the vale

# Backstory
After the starving winter in the Vale, Garland invested in a caravan outfit moving grain, salt, and other goods between Beaconhold and frontier territories. It was originally meant to guarantee the Vale could import food if another famine came, and it did provide a valuable tie to the outside world for decades.

Their routes haven’t entered the vale in some time, but Garland felt secure knowing his ownership stake meant he could call on them if starvation ever closed in again.

In time, the single caravan expanded into a major guild with an expansive overland trade network.

# Modern Situation
- Garland technically owns an ancient founding share of the company.
- He has documents to prove his ownership and a founder’s challenge coin.
- The company is now run by a wealthy merchant family who assumed the silent partner died generations ago.

# Potential NPC angle
Cassian Vellor, ambitious second son of one of the current directors, has become aware that someone appeared at the home office recently with papers supposedly proving his stake in the company, which initially caused quite a stir. The old man asked for very little though, and has thus far been allowed to draw down on petty funds when visiting Starfall trading posts.

Eager to prove his worth, Cassian suspects that Garland is a fraud, some kind of necromancer wearing the founder’s identity, or maybe even something worse than that, and he’s out to prove it
